⛵ Knots ⇄ mph ⇄ km/h

One knot is one nautical mile per hour. A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 m, so 1 kn = 1.852 km/h ≈ 1.15078 mph. Used by ships, aircraft and marine forecasts.
