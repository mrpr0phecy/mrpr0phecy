# staff/ — the owner's planning notes

These are notes, not a system. The "AI Developer staff" machinery that used to
live here (profiles, a scoreboard, a claims ledger, an audit engine, a
scheduled workflow and ~3 s of regression tests in every verify run) was
deleted on 2026-09-20: it produced governance about governance and no visitor
ever saw any of it. Nothing in `scripts/`, `verify.sh` or CI reads this folder.

What is still worth reading:

- `BOARD.md` — the working backlog, oldest entries at the top.
- `OPEN.md` — ideas that were investigated and deliberately not shipped, each
  with the reason and what would have to be true to revisit it.
- `DECISIONS.md` — decisions that are still binding, with their dates.
- `NORTH-STAR.md` — what the site is for, and the metric that matters.
- `RESEARCH.md` — keyword, audience and competitor research behind the tools.
- `GROWTH-PACK.md` — the growth levers that are allowed (see `INCOME.md`).
- `OPERATING-PLAN.md`, `OWNER-PACKET.md` — how the week is run, and the
  one-page summary for the owner.

Add to them freely. Nothing checks the format, and nothing fails if a note is
stale — that is the point.
