House rules

A few things I won't do, whatever the offer:

  • No third-party scripts. Your placement is static HTML I add myself. Nothing of yours runs in a visitor's browser.
  • No tracking pixels. Nothing in your placement collects anything, and no visitor-level data is shared with anyone.
  • Always labelled Sponsored. Never disguised as part of a tool or as a recommendation.
  • Nothing that makes a tool worse — no layout shift, no delay, no interruption of the thing the visitor came to do.
  • Products I'd be comfortable recommending. No gambling, no crypto speculation, no MLM, no "AI girlfriend", no scams.
