Straight answers

Who exactly do I clear with?

One person: the artist. Master and publishing are both held by MrProphecy, so there is no label, no publisher and no co-writer split to negotiate. The agreement is between you and the rights-holder directly.

Can I use a track in a monetised YouTube video?

Yes — that's the Creator tier. You get written permission so there's no ambiguity, and the track won't be claimed against you for the licensed use.

What about Content ID and streaming?

Licensed uses are documented in the agreement with project, media and term, so any automated claim can be released with the paperwork. Tell me upfront if the project needs whitelisting anywhere.

Can I get an exclusive?

For commissions, yes — buyout terms available. For catalogue tracks, exclusivity is unusual and priced accordingly; ask and you'll get an honest answer about whether it's even sensible for your project.

What won't you license for?

Gambling, crypto speculation, payday lending, political campaigns, and anything hateful or deceptive. Same house rules as the rest of this site — the catalogue's reputation is worth more than any single fee.
