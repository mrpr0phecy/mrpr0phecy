❓ Questions, answered

What does one-stop licensing mean?

One person holds 100% of the rights — music, visuals, everything — so clearance is a single conversation and a one-page licence. No co-writers, no label, no publisher, no six-month chain.

How much does it cost to licence a track?

Typical ranges: £50–£500 for YouTube and indie uses, £500–£5,000+ for adverts and games. Every quote is confirmed by email before anything is final.

What files do I get?

WAV plus instrumental as standard, trackouts on request, and the one-page licence naming your project, term and territory.
