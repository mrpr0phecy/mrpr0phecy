Jobs: one form, several tools, the numbers carried forward

A single tool answers a single question. These 8 jobs answer the one you actually had — and every step is a normal tool on this site, opened with your numbers already in it.

How a job works

  1. Open the first step and fill in what it asks.
  2. The rail above the tool shows where you are. Press Next step — it reads the answer you just got and puts it into the next tool.
  3. Everything travels in the web address: tool.html?card=…&field=value&run=1. Nothing is stored, nothing is uploaded, and every step can be shared as a link on its own.

Hand-built, not scraped: each job is checked against the 21 tools it uses on every build, so a renamed field fails the build instead of shipping a link that fills in nothing.

1. Can I afford this home?

Start with what you earn and what you have saved, end with the monthly payment and what overpaying would save.

  1. 1 What a lender might offer Your income, deposit, term and the rate you expect. The result is a borrowing range, not a promise. carries Deposit available into mortgage-down, Rate you expect to pay into mortgage-rate, Mortgage term into mortgage-years
  2. 2 The monthly payment The deposit, rate and term you just used are already in — add the price you are looking at. carries Interest Rate into mo-rate, Term Length into mo-term
  3. 3 What overpaying saves Your rate and term carried over. Add the balance you would still owe.

2. From your weight to what you eat

Height, weight and age typed once, then carried through BMI, daily energy needs and a macro split.

  1. 1 Start with BMI Height, weight, age. Nothing leaves the page. carries Height in centimetres into bmr-height, Height in centimetres into bmr-height-input, Weight in kilograms into bmr-weight, Weight in kilograms into bmr-weight-input, Age into bmr-age, Age into bmr-age-input
  2. 2 Your daily energy needs Height, weight and age are in. Pick the activity level that matches an ordinary week. carries height input into mc-height, weight input into mc-weight, age input into mc-age, your daily energy figure into mc-custom-calories
  3. 3 Split it into protein, carbs and fat Your daily calorie figure is carried in; set the split you want to try.

3. Measure once, decorate the room

Type the room, paint, wallpaper and flooring all get the same dimensions — no re-measuring, no unit slips.

  1. 1 Paint the walls Width, length and ceiling height, plus coats and tin size. carries Room Width into wallpap-w, Room Length into wallpap-l, Ceiling Height into wallpap-h
  2. 2 Work out the wallpaper Your room is already in. Add doors, windows and the pattern repeat. carries Room Width into tileflr-w, Room Length into tileflr-l
  3. 3 And the floor Same room again. Pick the tile size, pack coverage and waste allowance.

4. A UK recipe, in an air fryer

Scale the recipe, turn its gas mark into degrees, then into air-fryer temperature and time.

  1. 1 Scale the recipe Original and new servings, and the units your recipe uses. no numbers to carry — the next step starts fresh
  2. 2 Gas mark to degrees Read the temperature from your recipe and put it in here (or pick the gas mark). carries °C into afrk-c
  3. 3 Convert it for the air fryer The temperature is in. Add the time the recipe gives.

7. From an emergency fund to investing

Work out what to put aside each month, see it build up, then see what compounding does to it.

  1. 1 What should you put aside? Essential monthly spend and a deadline — and, in "or monthly budget", what you can actually save. carries …or monthly budget into savings-monthly
  2. 2 Watch it build up Your monthly amount is in. Add what you already have and an interest rate. carries Monthly Deposit into ci-monthly-contribution
  3. 3 What compounding does The same monthly amount, invested. Try different periods and rates.

Honest limits

A job carries numbers you typed, and figures a tool has already worked out — it never guesses. Where a step needs something no earlier step produced (a mortgage balance, a pattern repeat, the time on your recipe card), the step says so and you type it.

Nothing about a job runs without you: no card is executed in the background, and the site cannot see which job you opened. Started a job and stopped? Just reopen any step — the URL keeps that step's numbers.

Want the machine-readable version? jobs.json describes every step, every carried value and where it goes. Agents and developers: agents.html.
