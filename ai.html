Knowledge base

Retrieval over documents you supply — nothing is pre-loaded and nothing is uploaded. Text is chunked, indexed with BM25 and 64-dimension hashed embeddings, and ranked with optional HyDE expansion, reciprocal rank fusion and MMR diversity.

Memory

Generative-agents style recall: every memory is scored by relevance (cosine over hashed embeddings), recency (exponential forgetting curve) and importance (1–10), then stored in IndexedDB on this device. Consolidation writes reflection summaries over recent memories.

Model & privacy

This system works with no model at all: the structural engine reasons over your documents, memory and local tools. Loading a language model is optional, happens only when you press the button, and runs the weights on this device through WebGPU.

Local model

Loading downloads model weights from the runtime's CDN into your browser cache — typically 0.4–2 GB. It is the only network traffic this page ever generates, and it only starts when you press the button.

What is stored, and where

Honest limits

  • No model, no prose. Without a loaded model the structural engine will not pretend to write free-form text. It decomposes, retrieves, computes, verifies and says plainly what it could not do.
  • Small local models are weak. A 0.5–3 GB on-device model is far below a hosted frontier model. Traces are shown so you can judge the answer rather than trust it.
  • Retrieval is lexical + hashed vectors. BM25 and 64-dimension hashed embeddings are fast and private, but they are not a trained semantic encoder.
  • Not advice. Medical, legal and financial output is general information. Check anything that matters with a professional.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no account. There is nothing to phone home to; the only optional request is the model download above.

Methods implemented, and the papers behind them

A standalone AI system. It is not part of any tool catalogue on this domain, it recommends no external tools, and it has no API — everything happens in this tab.

Reasoning and retrieval methods: Chain-of-Thought (Wei 2022) · Self-Consistency (Wang 2022) · Tree-of-Thought (Yao 2023) · ReAct (Yao 2022) · Reflexion (Shinn 2023) · Step-Back (Zheng 2023) · Least-to-Most (Zhou 2022) · Graph-of-Thought (Besta 2023) · Buffer-of-Thoughts (Zhou 2023) · Self-Discover (Zhou 2024) · Quiet-STaR (Zelikman 2024) · Algorithm-of-Thoughts (Selj 2023) · Chain-of-Verification (Dhuliawala 2023) · Self-Refine (Madaan 2023) · Cumulative Reasoning (Zhang 2023) · Mixture-of-Agents (Wang 2024) · Meta-prompting (Suzgun 2024) · Constitutional AI (Bai 2022) · HyDE (Gao 2022) · RRF (Cormack 2009) · MMR (Carbonell 1998) · Generative Agents (Park 2023) · MemGPT (Packer 2023).
