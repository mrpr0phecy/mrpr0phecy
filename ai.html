Model & privacy

Lantern works with no model at all. Loading one is optional, happens only when you press the button, and runs the weights on this device through WebGPU.

This device

checking…

On-device model

not loaded

Loading downloads weights into your browser cache — typically 0.4–2 GB. It is the only network traffic this page can generate, and it only starts when you press the button.

Honest limits

  • No model, no invention. Without a model Lantern composes answers from your documents, your memory and computed tool results. It does not write free-form prose and it says what it could not do.
  • Small local models are weak. A 0.4–2 GB on-device model is far below a hosted frontier model. The working is always shown so you can judge the answer rather than trust it.
  • Retrieval is lexical plus hashed vectors. BM25 and 64-dimension hashed embeddings are fast and private, but they are not a trained semantic encoder.
  • Not advice. Medical, legal and financial output is general information. Check anything that matters with a professional.
  • Nothing leaves this device. No account, no cookies and no analytics on this page; the only optional request is the model download above.

Where things are stored

Documents, memory, preferences, ratings and conversations live in this browser's IndexedDB and localStorage — for this origin only. Clearing site data, or the button below, removes them for good.

The methods, and the papers behind them

Lantern is a standalone AI: it fetches no catalogue, recommends no other service, has no API, and keeps everything in this tab.

Reasoning and retrieval methods: Chain-of-Thought (Wei 2022) · Self-Consistency (Wang 2022) · Tree-of-Thought (Yao 2023) · ReAct (Yao 2022) · Reflexion (Shinn 2023) · Step-Back (Zheng 2023) · Least-to-Most (Zhou 2022) · Graph-of-Thought (Besta 2023) · Buffer-of-Thoughts (Zhou 2023) · Self-Discover (Zhou 2024) · Quiet-STaR (Zelikman 2024) · Algorithm-of-Thoughts (Sel 2023) · Chain-of-Verification (Dhuliawala 2023) · Self-Refine (Madaan 2023) · Cumulative Reasoning (Zhang 2023) · Mixture-of-Agents (Wang 2024) · Meta-prompting (Suzgun 2024) · Constitutional AI (Bai 2022) · HyDE (Gao 2022) · RRF (Cormack 2009) · MMR (Carbonell 1998) · Generative Agents (Park 2023) · MemGPT (Packer 2023).
