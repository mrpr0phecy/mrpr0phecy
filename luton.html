A One-Person Studio

MrProphecy is a rapper, producer and animator from Luton. There is no team, no label and no studio budget behind this — the writing, the production, the mixing and the animation all come from the same desk.

That's unusual. Most artists with animated videos commission them. Here the person who wrote the verse also drew the frames, which is why the visuals track the music so closely.

The roots are in Sons of South, the UK hip hop collective behind the All Eyes On The South mixtapes.

The Luton Sound

What comes out of a town twenty-five miles north of London that isn't trying to sound like London.

🎤 UK hip hop first

British accents, British references, no transatlantic affect. The crew years set that and it stuck.

🎛️ Genre-agnostic

Deep house, UK garage, soca, ambient, electronic. The catalogue moves wherever the track needs to go.

🎬 Visual by default

Animation isn't promotion for the music, it's part of it. That's the thing Luton produced that nowhere else did.

Common Questions

Who is MrProphecy?

A rapper, producer and animator based in Luton, Bedfordshire. The writing, the beats, the mixing and the animation all come from one desk.

Where is MrProphecy from?

Luton, Bedfordshire. Not London — Luton. The whole catalogue is made here.

What genre is it?

Rooted in UK hip hop, but the catalogue crosses into deep house, UK garage, soca and electronic. Tracks like Inhale sit in deep house; Infiltrate is dark UK garage.

Where can I listen for free?

Everywhere. 47 animated videos on YouTube, the audio archive on SoundCloud. No signup, no subscription, no paywall.

What is Sons of South?

The UK hip hop collective behind the All Eyes On The South mixtapes. MrProphecy came up with the crew and still produces for them.
