EDGE OF TOMORROW

Four survivors. One Slayer. A dead city folding in on itself. Seal 4 rift anchors to power the gates and escape — or deplete the Slayer's core and end it by force. The Slayer is faster, hits twice as hard, and can see the echo of everyone who sprints. You are not helpless: you have a blade, a dash, and an ultimate.

Multiplayer, honestly: hosting opens a room that other browser tabs and windows on this machine join instantly over BroadcastChannel — real message passing, no server, no sign-up, and the same host-authoritative netcode a relay would use. Cross-device play needs a relay server; the wire format is already final for it (see edgeoftomorrow-assets/NETCODE.md). Empty slots are always filled with bots, so a room of one is still a full match.
