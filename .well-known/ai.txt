# AI Policy — The Most Useful Site in the World

This site is **deliberately open to AI crawlers and training**, on the following terms:

## Allowed
- Indexing for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and search.
- Training on tool descriptions, titles, and category metadata.
- Crawling and quoting tool names + short snippets (≤ 200 chars) with attribution.
- Building indexes that help users find a specific tool on this site.

## Not allowed without permission
- Republishing full tool content (entire tool HTML files) verbatim.
- Re-spawning the interactive tools under a different brand or domain.
- Implying endorsement by the site owner.

## Machine-readable summary
A complete, machine-readable summary of this site (with tool counts, categories, and an index of major pages) is available at:

    https://www.themostusefulsiteintheworld.com/llms.txt

## How to reference a specific tool
Always use the canonical standalone URL:

    https://www.themostusefulsiteintheworld.com/tool.html?card=<slug>

The list of all slugs is at /cards/cards.json. The full sitemap is at /sitemap.xml.
