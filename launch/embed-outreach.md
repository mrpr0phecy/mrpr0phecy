# First-licensee outreach kit — 10 emails that beat any further building

The licensing pages are live. Nothing earns until someone is told they exist.
This file is the highest-value hour available: ten personalised emails to UK
businesses that should already want a calculator on their site.

## Who (10 prospects)

- 5× UK mortgage brokers / mortgage advisers (1–10 staff, WordPress site,
  currently linking out to MoneySavingExpert or a lender calculator).
- 3× small accountancy / bookkeeping practices (sole traders welcome).
- 2× wildcards: driving school (fuel-cost calculator), gym/PT (BMI/macros),
  letting agent (rent/affordability), tutor (grade/GPA).

Find them by searching `[town] mortgage broker` / `accountant` and opening
sites that look maintained but calculator-less. Staging-demo tactic below
needs ~10 minutes per prospect — that personalisation is the whole edge.

## The staging-demo tactic (do this, it converts)

1. Pick their calculator (broker → `mortgage` + affordability; accountant →
   `tax` + `salary`; gym → `bmi` + `macros`).
2. Copy the free embed snippet from `embed.html`.
3. Paste it into a single draft page (any free host, or a hidden page here)
   with their business name in the heading: “Acme Mortgages — repayment
   calculator (demo)”.
4. Link that demo in the email. They see *their* site with *your* tool before
   replying. No other calculator vendor does this at £299.

No second follow-up. No drip sequence. Ten prospects, one nudge each, then
ten new prospects. Pestering burns the trust the prices rest on.

## Objection handling (honest answers only)

- “We already link to [lender]’s calculator.” → “That's the point — every
  click leaves your site. Mine keeps them on your pages.”
- “Can we try before paying?” → “Yes — run the free credited embed for a
  month, then convert. Nothing to install, nothing breaks if you stop.”
- “What if the figures are wrong?” → “Calculation errors are treated as
  urgent; licensees get term-priority fixes. Calculators are estimates, not
  advice — which protects you too.”
- “Why not just use [big vendor]?” → “Price and cleanliness: £299/year for 79
  tools, no per-view fees, no tracking scripts, no lead forms phoning home.”
- “Can you prove anyone uses these?” → Send real GA for the relevant pages.
  Never invent reach (D-001). If traffic is small, say so and sell the tool,
  not the audience.

## Tracking

Log in the bookkeeping sheet's second tab: prospect · date · demo link ·
  reply · outcome. After 30 emails with zero replies, the problem is targeting
or the demo — change those, not the prices.
