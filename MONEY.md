## 0. The model in 30 seconds

**Free tools and free music are the acquisition channel. The money is in
selling to businesses and commercial users — never in gating the free stuff.**

| # | Revenue line | Type | Realistic first-year shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Embed licences** (`license.html`) | Recurring (£99–£899/yr) | 4 category licences = £1,196/yr renewing. 20 = £5,980. Traffic-independent. |
| 2 | **Custom builds + audits** (`hire.html`) | One-off (£149–£1,000) | 1–2 builds/month = £6k–£24k/yr. Highest £/hour after sync. |
| 3 | **Sync licensing** (`sync.html`) | One-off (£50–£5,000+) | One £500 placement beats a year of donations. Best £/hour on the music side. |
| 4 | **Sponsorship** (`sponsor.html`) | Recurring (£49–£199+/mo) | Second string for tools; needs traffic proof per page. |
| 5 | **YouTube Partner Programme** | Recurring (ad share) | Closest to passive; needs the watch-hour check + 1 Feb 2027 deadline. |
| 6 | **Donations** (`donate.html`, `support.html`) | One-off (£3–£25) | Real but small (~0.01–0.1% conversion). Do not over-optimise. |
| 7 | **Digital products** (Phase 2, not built) | One-off (£19–£49) | Offline pack, money toolkit, stems pack. Needs real artefacts first — spec in §8. |

Nothing here gates a tool or a track. That is the positioning the prices rest
on: buyers pay for *their* site's branding, for correctness, and for the
builder's time — not for access.

### The legally load-bearing distinction (do not blur this)

- **Code on GitHub is MIT** — anyone may self-host, adapt and brand it under
  that licence's terms (keep the copyright notice). This is genuine and is
  stated on `license.html`, `help.html` and `legal.html`.
- **Hosted embeds (iframes served from this domain) are a service** — free on
  condition the credit line stays visible and followable; removal needs a
  licence. This is in `legal.html` §5 and the snippet itself.
- The licence therefore sells the **hosted, maintained, supported, branded**
  version — not permission to use open code. That framing is what makes the
  prices honest and the terms enforceable. Never write copy that implies
  otherwise.

### Instrumentation (no footprint change on clean pages)

- New money pages (`license.html`, `hire.html`, `sync.html`) carry the same
  inline GA tag as the existing money pages (`donate.html`, `sponsor.html`).
  Owner-authorised: they are the same category of page and the funnel cannot

- Funnel events (fire only where `gtag` already exists — no new loaders):
  `embed_copy` (+ tool slug), `embed_licence_cta`, `licence_*`, `hire_*`,
  `sync_*`. In GA: which tools get embedded → which tiers get clicked.

## 2. Pricing rationale (so future edits don't drift)

- **£99 single / £299 category / £899 full** — set in `STRATEGY.md` against
  published competitors (CalcWidgets $79/mo, Outgrow $22–115/mo, mortgage
  toolboxes £79–179/yr for 8 calculators). We are 5–10× cheaper than the
  market for 10–100× the inventory. Four £299 licences beat a year of
  plausible sponsorship and renew.
- **£49/mo single sponsor / £199/mo category** — starter anchors, not a rate
  card. Confirmed against real per-page GA before quoting; the page says so.
  Raise when a page has proof, never before.
- **£149 audit / from £495 custom / £1,000 flagship / £79/mo care** — the
  audit is the wedge (cheap, fast, proves competence); custom is mid-market
  freelance for 1–2 weeks; flagship preserves the previously published £1,000
  offer (don't silently undercut published prices — extend the ladder instead).
- **Sync £50–500 / £500–5,000+** — per `INCOME.md` unsigned-artist ranges.
  Ranges, not fixed fees: every licence is quoted per project in writing.

## 3. Payments — the setup still needed (owner actions)

The site is static (no backend), so checkout is **hosted payment links**.
Every buy CTA currently points at a `mailto:` with a pre-filled subject/body —
that works today and stays as the fallback/enquiry route. To take card
payments without emails:

1. **Stripe Payment Links** (recommended for licences + sponsorship):
   - Create one link per product: Single £99/yr, Category £299/yr,
     Full £899/yr, Sponsor-single £49/mo, Sponsor-category £199/mo.
   - Annual, auto-renew, GBP. Stripe handles VAT receipts.
   - Replace each `<!-- BUY: … -->` mailto in `license.html`/`sponsor.html`
     with the link. Keep a “prefer email?” fallback line.
2. **PayPal** stays for donations/tips (already live) and can take licence
   payments manually via invoice while Stripe is unset.
3. **Fulfilment is manual and tiny**: on payment, email the branded snippet +
   confirm the tier/sites in writing (one paragraph). Log it in the
   bookkeeping sheet (§7). No licence keys, no DRM — the terms + the
   relationship are the enforcement, which is proportionate at this scale.
4. **Do not** add Stripe/PayPal *scripts* to tool pages or embeds. Links only.
   Third-party checkout scripts on Product A would break the privacy
   positioning the licences are sold on.

## 4. The first licensee (the only task that converts this into money)

Ranked by expected return per hour. Do these in order; stop building and do
#1 first.

1. **Ten emails.** UK mortgage brokers + small accountancy practices. Template
   + follow-up in `launch/embed-outreach.md`. The winning move: paste a
   working embed of *their* calculator into a one-page demo on your staging
   and link it — they see their site with your tool before replying.
2. **One vertical push.** The finance spotlight on `license.html` + `embed.html`
   already target “mortgage calculator for my website”. Next: ask two friendly
   brokers to trial a free credited embed for a month, then convert.
3. **Prove maintenance publicly.** Every April: apply statutory updates, note
   them in `changelog.html` (“2026/27 bands applied 6 April”), link that entry
   from `license.html`. That changelog is the trust asset for licence buyers.
4. **Instrument review, 30 days in.** GA: top `embed_copy` tools → those are
   the licence prospects; top `licence_*` clicks → tune that tier's copy.
5. **Sync outreach in parallel.** Five emails to indie-game devs / YouTubers /
   small agencies with a 5-track shortlist each. One placement can exceed a
   year of donations.

## 5. What deliberately stays free / refused (reaffirmed)

- **No display ads on tools.** Would earn pounds and destroy the differentiator
  the licences are sold on. (Unchanged from `INCOME.md`/`STRATEGY.md`.)
- **No paywalling tools or music.** The free catalogue is the shop window.
- **No lead-capture or tracking in embeds.** Competitors sell on this; our
  compliance-safe “nothing leaves the browser” is the counter-position.
- **No crypto/token monetisation.** `token.html` is kept but not promoted;
  unresolved FSMA exposure — see `FINANCE.md` §3.
- **No fake urgency, fake counts, view-bots, hidden players.** Ever. The
  prices depend on trust; trust depends on true numbers (D-001).
- **No sponsor influence over tool output.** Sold placements are labelled
  lines under tools, placed by hand, one per page max.

restored or the slot cleared within 30 days — no kill-switches, no ransom.
Say so on the pages (already said) and mean it.

## 7. Bookkeeping (minimum viable, from FINANCE.md)

1. Separate PayPal balance / account for site+music money.
2. Spreadsheet: date · source · gross · fees · net. Every receipt.
3. Running total vs the **£1,000 trading allowance** (gross, all sources
   combined), checked quarterly. Cross it → register for Self Assessment by
   **5 October** after the tax year ends.
4. Keep records 5 years past the 31 January deadline.
5. Sync/licence fees are the events that push totals over thresholds
   unexpectedly — log them the day they land, not in January.
