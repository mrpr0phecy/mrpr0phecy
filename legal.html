2. Privacy — what data is and isn't collected

The honest position, stated precisely:

  • There is no server and no database. Every tool in the catalogue runs entirely inside your browser. Anything you type into a calculator, converter, generator or game is processed on your device and is never transmitted to the site owner.
  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no logins. Nothing to register for, so no name, email or password is ever held.
  • No advertising network and no ad tracking anywhere on the site. No data is ever sold, rented or shared for marketing.
  • Some tools save your work locally using your browser's localStorage — for example tool ratings, budget entries or game scores. That data stays on your device, is readable only by this site in that browser, and disappears when you clear your browser storage. It is never uploaded.
  • Analytics are optional and off by default — see §3.

Hosting logs. The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes standard technical request data (including IP address) to deliver pages and to protect the service. The site owner has no access to those logs and cannot identify visitors from them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Email. If you email the address above, that email and any reply are kept for as long as needed to deal with your message and any follow-up, then deleted.

Children. The site is general-audience and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including under-13s.

4. Your data rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and port your personal data. In practice, because the site holds almost nothing:

  • Erase everything this site knows about you locally: clear site data for this domain in your browser settings. That removes all saved tool data and your consent choice.
  • Opt out of analytics: click the button in §3 and choose Decline.
  • Anything else: email hello@themostusefulsiteintheworld.com and it will be handled within one month, free of charge.

If you are unhappy with the response you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
