**Why these guides exist.** The internet used to have a million small useful tools, and most of them were on a personal homepage. The modern search results for "calculate mortgage" or "what's my BMI" are dominated by sites that exist to harvest an email address, sign you up for a newsletter, or sell your output as a lead to a lender. There is no business model here. There is no newsletter, no lead form, no upsell, no pop-up. The whole point of the site is that the calculation is the product.
