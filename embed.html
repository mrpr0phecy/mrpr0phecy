Every tool on this site is a standalone page that works inside an iframe. Copy the snippet, paste it into your CMS, and the tool is live on your site — no JavaScript, no API key, no sign-up, no tracking, no paywall.

📋 How to embed

1. Pick a tool below.

2. Click "Copy iframe" — the snippet is on your clipboard.

3. Paste into your site, blog, Notion page, or anywhere else that accepts HTML. You can adjust the width and height attributes.

Default snippet (height 450px):

<iframe src="https://www.themostusefulsiteintheworld.com/tool.html?card=TOOL-SLUG" width="100%" height="450" style="border:none;border-radius:12px;" title="TOOL TITLE" loading="lazy"></iframe>
