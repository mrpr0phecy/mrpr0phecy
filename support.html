Where It Actually Goes

There's no label and no team. Money in goes to the things that cost money: hosting, software licences, rendering time, and the occasional piece of gear when something breaks.

Nothing gets unlocked. Supporters don't get exclusive tracks, early access or a private feed — because that would mean holding music back from everyone else, and the whole point is that it's free.

It's not expected. Listening is the point. If money's tight, subscribe and share instead — that genuinely helps more than a one-off fiver.
