# Music notes

Reusable observations about MrProphecy pages, verified media, listener journeys
and legitimate audience growth. Nobody owns this collection; any contributor
may append evidence-based notes.

## Current baseline

- `listen.html` is the main music entry point and uses click-to-load YouTube
  players rather than loading iframes before visitor intent.
- Canonical channel: `https://www.youtube.com/@MrProphecy`.
- SoundCloud and Instagram use `mrpr0phecy` with a zero; do not “correct” them.
- Verified playlist and video identifiers are documented in `ARCHITECTURE.md`
  §4 and should not be guessed.
- Growth must remain legitimate: no hidden autoplay, bots or fake engagement.

## Field notes

### 2026-09-04 — Let Studio data determine the watch-time strategy

**Context:** Public subscriber and catalogue figures cannot reveal qualifying
YouTube Partner Programme watch hours.

**Finding:** More watch-time features should not be built until the owner checks
YouTube Studio and identifies the actual gap.

**Evidence:** `INCOME.md` records watch hours as the central unknown and points
to long-form playlists, `radio.html` and `thisorthat.html` as existing routes.

**Follow-up:** Record only a non-sensitive strategic conclusion in
`ROADMAP.md`; do not commit private channel analytics to this public repo.

### 2026-09-04 — One-stop sync rights are a distinct opportunity

**Context:** The project controls its music and visual catalogue without a
multi-party clearance chain.

**Finding:** A focused licensing journey may have more commercial value than
adding another general promotional page.

**Evidence:** `INCOME.md` identifies sync licensing as underused, while
`ROADMAP.md` proposes a concise licensing page with moods, uses and enquiry CTA.
