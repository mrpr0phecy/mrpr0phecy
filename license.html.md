## What you're actually buying

Not the HTML — anyone can write a mortgage calculator. You're buying **maintained correctness**:

**UK money figures move every April.** Tax bands, National Insurance thresholds, student loan thresholds — and the maths is easy to get subtly wrong (the £100k personal-allowance taper creates a 60% marginal rate that ready-made calculators routinely miss).

- **April reviews.** Statutory figures are re-checked every tax year; the licence guarantees it happens inside your term.
- **Tested maths.** Money tools are covered by automated correctness checks that run before anything ships.
- **Nothing to install.** One iframe. No plugin to update, no dependency to patch, no Cookie-banner implications from the embed itself.
- **Clean for compliance.** No third-party scripts, no pixels, no lead forms phoning home. What a visitor types stays in their browser — a feature for *your* privacy posture too.

## Questions

**Is the free tier really free for commercial sites?**

Yes. Put any tool on a business page, a client site, a course, a newsletter landing page — free, forever. The only condition is the small credit line under the embed, exactly as the copied snippet includes it.

**What counts as “removing” the credit line?**

Hiding it with CSS, shrinking it to nothing, covering it, or deleting it from the snippet. If a visitor can't see and follow it, it's removed — and that needs a licence. This is in the [terms of use](https://www.themostusefulsiteintheworld.com/legal.html#terms).

**Do the embeds track my visitors or slow my site?**

Nothing to track with — the embed loads zero third-party scripts, sets zero cookies and sends zero beacons. The iframe lazy-loads and the tools run locally in the visitor's browser, so there is no per-view cost to anyone and nothing to leak. No lead-capture either: if a visitor wants more, the credit line brings them to the free tool here.

**What happens if a figure is wrong?**

Email it in. Calculation errors are treated as urgent on the public tools for everyone; licensees get term-priority fixes. And to be clear about what this is: the calculators are estimates for illustration — **not financial advice**, not tax advice, and not a substitute for a qualified adviser. That protects you as much as it protects this site.

**Can I self-host instead?**

The site's code is MIT licensed on GitHub, so you can always self-host and brand it yourself for free — that's genuine, not a bluff. The licence buys you the hosted, maintained, supported version: no merging upstream fixes, no April fire-drill, one email when something needs changing.

**What if I cancel?**

Your embeds keep working — nothing breaks on your pages. You either restore the credit line (back to the free tier) or remove the embeds within 30 days. No lock-in, no kill-switch, no ransom.
