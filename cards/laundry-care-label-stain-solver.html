Three rules that prevent 90% of laundry disasters

1) Sort darks from whites — one red sock turns a whole load pink. 2) Never tumble-dry a stain — heat bakes it in permanently. 3) Less detergent beats more — excess leaves residue, dulls colours and makes towels scratchy.
