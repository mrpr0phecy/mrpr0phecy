The physics

Earth: crust, silicate mantle, liquid iron outer core, solid inner core. P waves (Vp ≈ 8 km/s in the upper mantle) refract at every boundary and cross the core. S waves (Vs ≈ Vp/√3) cannot shear a liquid, so they vanish at the core–mantle boundary — stations between ~104° and 140° from the epicentre sit in the S-wave shadow. Surface (Rayleigh/Love) waves travel the crust at ~3.5 km/s and usually carry the damage. Amplitude on the traces scales as 10^(0.5 Mw), Gutenberg–Richter style.
