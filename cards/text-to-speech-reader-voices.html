🔊 Text-to-Speech Reader (Offline Browser Voices)

Paste any text and have your device read it aloud using the voices already built into your browser or operating system — no upload, no account. Choose voice and language, adjust speed and pitch, follow along with word highlighting, and pause or skip between sentences. Great for proofreading, accessibility and language practice.

Voice quality depends on your device. On Windows try the "Natural" voices, on macOS/iOS "Siri" or "Enhanced" voices (download in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content). Chrome's Google voices need an internet connection; the rest work offline.
