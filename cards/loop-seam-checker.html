Analyzes the entire decoded file as one loop. A large jump is a click risk, not a guaranteed audible click. Start/end silence and compressed-codec padding can affect results. This checks boundaries, not tempo or musical phrasing; move loop points to similar waveform positions or apply a short crossfade in your DAW.
