    Why airbeds feel freezing at 3 AM — and the Christmas Eve Living-Room Rule

    The convective airbed trap: Inflatable air mattresses contain zero insulation; cold floor air circulates inside the PVC chamber and pulls body heat straight downward, making guests shiver even under a 13.5-tog duvet. Always lay a folded thick blanket or yoga mat underneath an airbed AND a mattress protector or spare duvet between the vinyl top and the fitted sheet. Inflate the airbed 3 hours before bedtime and top it up for 15 seconds right before sleep once the PVC stretches!

    The 6 AM Living-Room Rule: Never put a late-sleeping teenager or solo adult on the main Christmas-tree living-room sofa bed if young children are in the house — excited kids will charge into the room at 6:15 AM! Put children in a bedroom floor-camp together and reserve any downstairs study or dining room for solo adult overflow. With 1 bathroom for 8+ people (`~11 min` per person), move half the household's showers to Christmas Eve night so hot water cylinders recover cleanly.
