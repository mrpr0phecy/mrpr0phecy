🥇 Athletics Decathlon & Heptathlon Points

The official World Athletics scoring tables: type a performance in any event and get its points, then watch the total build toward a world-class score. Times in seconds (or m:ss), jumps in centimetres, throws in metres.

The scoring formula

Points = floor( A × (B − P)C ) for running events (lower time is better) and floor( A × (P − B)C ) for jumps and throws (further is better), using the official 2001 World Athletics constants for each event. A 9,000-point decathlon or a 7,000-point heptathlon is world class.
