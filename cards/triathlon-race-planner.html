🏊‍♂️ Triathlon Race Planner

Build a race-day plan: pick a distance, enter your swim pace, bike speed, run pace and transitions, and this gives you the time for each leg and your finish line.

Notes

Transitions (T1 swim→bike, T2 bike→run) are included in your total time — a slick transition can save minutes over a season. These are flat-course, no-drafting pace assumptions; add a little for hills, chop or heat.
