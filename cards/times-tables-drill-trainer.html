Memory tricks
• Commutative: 7 × 8 is the same as 8 × 7 — half the facts to learn.
• ×9: the digits of the answer always add to 9 (9 × 7 = 63 → 6+3=9), or count down 9s on your fingers.
• ×11: up to 9×11, the answer is the digit repeated (4 × 11 = 44).
• ×5: halfway — 7 × 5 = 70 ÷ 2 = 35.
• Square numbers (1, 4, 9, 16…) anchor the grid: 6 × 6 = 36, then 6 × 7 = 42 is just one more 6.

Practice tip: short, frequent rounds beat long ones. Use the round summary — the facts you miss become your revision list.
