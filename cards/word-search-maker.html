🔤 Word Search Maker — Printable Puzzles with Answer Key (PDF)

Type your words and get a word search puzzle in seconds: choose the grid size and difficulty, preview it, then print or download a PDF with the puzzle and an answer key. Made in your browser, nothing uploaded.

How the puzzle is built, choosing a difficulty, and printing

How it is built. Words are placed longest first at random positions and directions, and a word may cross another wherever the letters agree — that is what makes a good puzzle, so the maker prefers crossings when it can. If a word will not fit, the grid grows a step (in Automatic mode) or the word is reported so you can shorten the list. Spaces, hyphens and accents are removed from the words for the grid but the word list prints them as you typed them, so “ice cream” is hidden as ICECREAM and listed as ice cream. The empty squares are then filled with random letters — or, on the harder setting, with letters drawn from your own words, which stops the eye from spotting a word by its unusual letters.

Choosing a difficulty. Easy hides words left-to-right and top-to-bottom only, which is right for early readers. Medium adds the two forward diagonals. Hard allows all eight directions, including backwards and upwards. Grid size matters as much: 10 × 10 with eight short words is a five-minute puzzle; 20 × 20 with twenty-five words fills a rainy afternoon. The maker picks a size automatically from the number and length of your words, and you can override it.

The puzzle code. Every arrangement has a short code. The same words with the same code give the same puzzle, so you can reprint it later or give a class the identical sheet; Shuffle picks a new code for a fresh arrangement of the same words. The code is printed in small type at the foot of the page.

Printing. The PDF is drawn in your browser at 150 dots per inch on A4 or US Letter, one page for the puzzle and one for the answer key (with every word circled), and nothing is sent to a server. Print opens the same PDF in a new tab for the browser’s print dialog; print at 100% so the squares stay square. Copy as text puts a monospaced grid and the word list on the clipboard for pasting into a document or message. Your words and settings are remembered in this browser only.
