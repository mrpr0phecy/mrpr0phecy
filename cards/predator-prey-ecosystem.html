The physics

Classic Lotka–Volterra: dx/dt = αx − βxy, dy/dt = δxy − γy. A logistic term x(1 − x/K) stops infinite hare blooms. The equilibrium is at x* = γ/δ, y* = α(1 − x*/K)/β. Trajectories orbit that point — delayed density dependence, the same reason lynx and snowshoe hare pelts in the Hudson Bay records swing on a ~10-year beat. The field is a spatial echo of those two numbers, not a second model.
