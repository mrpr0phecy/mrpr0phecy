    Bone-dry wood fibre contains ~4.95 kWh/kg across most temperate species, while density sets how much energy fits in one 25 cm log (oak ~1.45 kg/log vs pine ~0.95 kg/log). At moisture fraction m, net calorific value follows NCV ≈ 4.95·(1 − m) − 0.68·m kWh/kg because vaporising trapped sap consumes ~2.45 MJ/kg (0.68 kWh/kg) and cools flue gases into the sticky creosote condensation zone (<125 °C).
