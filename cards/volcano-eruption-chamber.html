🌋 Volcano Eruption Chamber

Magma viscosity is silica; explosivity is dissolved water flashing to steam. Low-silica Hawaiian fountains pour lava; high-silica, wet magma fragments into a Plinian column. VEI and column height follow Wilson/Sparks scaling from the mass eruption rate the chamber actually produces.

Raise silica and water to flip Hawaiian lava into a Plinian ash column.
The physics

Viscosity of silicate melts jumps ~10 orders of magnitude from 45% to 75% SiO₂ (Giordano–Russell–Dingwell). Dissolved water both drops viscosity and, on decompression, exsolves: if the bubble volume fraction exceeds ~70% the magma fragments. Mass eruption rate Ṁ ≈ ρ π r² u, column height H ≈ 0.3 Ṁ^0.25 (Sparks). VEI is a log scale of erupted volume; here it is inferred from Ṁ. Hawaiian, Strombolian, Vulcanian, sub-Plinian, Plinian follow from that pair of numbers.
