🧠 ADHD Time & Task Lab

Time blindness is not laziness, it is a broken odometer: the guess is wrong in a consistent direction, so it can be measured and corrected. Log a few estimate-vs-actual pairs and this learns your own multiplier, converts “it will only take ten minutes” into what it will really take, builds a ladder out of the stuck place, and runs a body-double timer. Nothing leaves your browser.

Say out loud what you are about to do before you press start — out loud beats in your head. A body double does not help, supervise or talk to you. They just sit there, and that is the whole trick.
