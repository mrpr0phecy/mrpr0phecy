      Why standard gingerbread cookies slump — and the trigonometry of roof shear

      Roof geometry & shear load: For a gable wall of width W, eaves height He and apex ridge height Hr, the gable rise is ΔH = Hr − He and the half-span is W / 2. By Pythagoras, the true sloped rafter length before overhang is R = √((W / 2)² + ΔH²), giving roof angle θ = arctan(2·ΔH / W). Adding 1.2 cm front/back and eaves overhangs keeps piped icicles clear of the walls. Heavy chocolate tiles increase shear force F_∥ = m·g·sin(θ) along the icing seam — always let the 4-wall chassis cure for 45–60 minutes (supported against soup cans!) before setting roof slabs.

      Zero-spread structural dough: Eating-grade gingerbread uses bicarbonate of soda or baking powder, which puffs and warps straight edges in the oven. Structural gingerbread omits chemical leaveners completely, rolls to a uniform 5 mm thickness between parchment sheets, chills 20 minutes before baking at `175 °C fan`, and trims cleanly with a pizza wheel while still hot from the oven. Isomalt or crushed boiled sweets can be spooned into window cutouts for last-5-minute stained-glass panes.
