🏷️ Business & Domain Name Generator

Type one or two words that describe what you do and get a few dozen names built from the tricks naming agencies actually use — blends, compounds, prefixes, suffixes, alliteration and invented words — each one scored for length, pronounceability and how well it will work as a handle. Favourites are saved to this browser, and the whole list exports as a file you can take to a registrar. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How the names are built, and what this tool cannot check

Names are assembled on the device from eight techniques: blends (half of one word plus half of another), compounds (two whole words joined), prefixes and suffixes drawn from a bank, alliteration with a themed noun, invented words built from pleasant syllable blocks, spelling plays (dropped vowels, swapped letters), Latinate endings that read as established, and place-style compounds that sound like a business with premises.

Each name is scored objectively where it can be. Length is measured in characters; syllables are estimated by counting vowel groups; pronounceability penalises three-letter consonant runs, missing vowels and awkward endings. Those are properties of the word. "Brandable" is not: that score is a heuristic on length, syllable count and pronounceability, and it cannot tell whether a name is any good.

It cannot check availability. Whether a domain, a company name or a social handle is free depends on live registers this tool deliberately does not query — doing so would mean sending your ideas to a third party. Take the shortlist to a registrar, search the companies register for your country, and check trademark databases before you commit. This is a naming aid, not legal or trademark advice.
