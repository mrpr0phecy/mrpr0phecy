🔤 Anagram Solver

Find every word you can make from your letters — from two letters up to the full anagram — with optional pattern matching.

Rules: only letters you type can be used, and each letter once. Use ? in the pattern for any letter (e.g. ?i??e? for six-letter words with an i at position 2 and e at position 5). A full anagram uses every letter exactly once. Results include words of three letters or more from a 6,000-word embedded English list.
