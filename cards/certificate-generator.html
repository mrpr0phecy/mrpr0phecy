Batch certificates, numbering, printing and the wording that works

Batches. Put one name per line in the Presented to box and every name gets its own page in the PDF — a class of thirty is one download. The arrows under the preview step through the names so you can check a long one still fits; names are shrunk automatically when they would run into the margins. If you add a certificate-number prefix, each page is numbered in order (NCC-2026-001, NCC-2026-002…) in the corner, which makes certificates easy to verify against your own register later.

Wording. The sentence after the name should read naturally as “for …”: for successfully completing…, for outstanding contribution to…, for attending…. Keep it to two lines; the date and the issuing organisation are printed separately, so there is no need to repeat them. Achievement is the safe default for courses and competitions, Appreciation for volunteers and speakers, Participation when everyone should get one.

Printing. Pages are landscape A4 or US Letter, rendered at 150 dots per inch, with a 12 mm margin so the frame is never clipped by a home printer. Heavier paper — 160 to 200 gsm, ivory or bright white — turns a printout into something people keep. Print at 100% (actual size); if you use a print shop, send the PDF as is. The PNG button saves the certificate on screen as an image for e-mailing or posting.

Privacy. Everything is drawn in your browser; names, the logo and the PDF never leave your device. Your wording and settings are remembered in this browser only, the logo is not.
