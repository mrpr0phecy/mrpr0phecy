🤸 Diving Score Calculator

Score a dive the way the judges do: enter the judges' marks and the degree of difficulty, and this drops the outliers and applies the official formula to give the dive its total.

How diving is scored

Judges mark each dive 0–10 in half-points. With 7 judges the two highest and two lowest marks are discarded and the three middle marks are summed; with 5 judges only the highest and lowest are dropped. That sum is multiplied by the dive's degree of difficulty (DD) for the total. (Synchronised diving uses a 0.6 adjustment — not applied here.)
