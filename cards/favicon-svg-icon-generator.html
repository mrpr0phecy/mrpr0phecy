🪄 Favicon & SVG Icon Generator

Make a sharp, lightweight vector favicon from initials or one emoji, tune its colours and shape, then download a ready-to-use SVG. It also gives you the HTML link tag and a data URI — no image upload, account or design app needed.

Use one emoji or up to three letters/numbers. SVG favicons scale cleanly at 16px through 512px.
SVG favicon compatibility

Modern browsers support SVG favicons and they stay crisp on high-density screens. For legacy clients that require .ico or PNG app icons, download this source and export a 16px, 32px, 180px or 512px raster version in an image editor. The SVG source remains the smallest, most flexible master file.
