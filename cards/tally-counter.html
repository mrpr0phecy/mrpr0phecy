      Tips, keys and what gets saved

      Counting fast. The big number is one large button, so you can tap it without looking. On a keyboard, focus it (click it once) and use Space, Enter, ↑ or + to add and ↓, − or Backspace to subtract. The full-screen clicker turns the whole screen into the add button with a smaller undo button at the bottom — handy for counting people through a door on a phone. Turn on “Keep screen awake” so the phone does not lock mid-count (supported browsers only).

      Several counters. Add a counter per thing you are tallying — cars and vans, male and female birds, knit and purl rows, visitors per door — and use the small − and + beside each one without switching. The total adds them all up. Step lets one tap count 2, 5 or 10 at a time; a target shows a progress bar and signals when you reach it.

      Log and rate. Every change is written to the log with the time, so you can see when things happened and undo mistakes with confidence. The rate is how many the selected counter went up by in the last five minutes, per minute and per hour — useful for traffic counts and footfall. Export CSV gives you the full log for a spreadsheet; counter names that begin with = + − or @ are prefixed with an apostrophe so a spreadsheet cannot run them as formulas.

      Privacy. Counters and the log are kept in this browser's storage on this device only (the newest 5,000 log lines). Nothing is uploaded; clearing your browser data removes them.
