Methodology (very serious)

This instrument applies the peer-reviewed principle that humans are, technically, a type of plant that walks around and needs sunlight. Readings are calibrated against the benchmark of “a golden retriever on a good day”. Accuracy not guaranteed; going outside is nonetheless recommended.
