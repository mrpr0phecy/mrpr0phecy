The rules
• Look at the digit one place to the right of where you’re rounding.
• 0–4 → round down (keep the digit). 5–9 → round up (add 1).
• 5 rounds up because the halfway value belongs to the bigger side.
• Estimate first: 187 × 42 ≈ 200 × 40 = 8,000. Exact: 7,854 — close enough to check your work.
• Money: £3.72 + £2.49 ≈ £4 + £2.50 = £6.50 (rough check: exact = £6.21).

Why estimate? a quick rounded check catches calculator slips and shopkeeper mistakes. If your exact answer is far from your estimate, something’s off.
