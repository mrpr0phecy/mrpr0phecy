Work out how many packs of laminate to buy: enter the room, the plank size, planks per pack and pack price, and this adds the wastage so you don't run short mid-lay.

Wastage & underlay

10% wastage covers offcuts, stagger joints and door trims — bump it to 15% for diagonal or herringbone layouts. Underlay is sold in rolls (typically 10 m²); order the room area plus the same 10%. Laminate is a floating floor: leave a 10 mm expansion gap at every wall.
