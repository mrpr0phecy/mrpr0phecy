🥩 Dog Food Safety & Toxicity Key

Emergency canine food safety encyclopedia. Search common household foods to identify lethal toxicities (Xylitol, grapes, alliums) versus safe nutrient-dense superfoods.

#1 Modern Killer: Xylitol (Birch Sugar / Wood Sugar) Found in sugar-free peanut butter, chewing gum, and baked goods. In dogs, Xylitol triggers a catastrophic 6-fold surge in insulin release within 30 minutes, causing severe hypoglycemic collapse, seizures, and acute liver failure. Always check peanut butter labels!
