    Tips, shortcuts and where the list lives

    Quick entry. Start a task with ! for high priority or !! for high priority and today's date. Type tomorrow, mon…sun or a date like 12/03 (day/month) at the end of a task to set its due date without touching the date box; a day/month that has already passed is taken to mean next year. Double-click (or press ✎) to edit a task in place; Enter saves, Esc cancels.

    Order. The ▲▼ buttons and drag-and-drop change "My order". The other sorts are views over the same list and do not throw your order away. Completed tasks always sink to the bottom.

    Due dates. Overdue tasks are marked in red, today's in gold. Dates are compared in your local time zone, and "Due today" also shows anything overdue so nothing hides.

    Storage. The list is saved in this browser's local storage every time it changes. It is not synced between devices and is deleted with "clear browsing data" — Export JSON gives you a file you can import here on another machine, and Copy as text produces a Markdown checklist for a message or a document.
