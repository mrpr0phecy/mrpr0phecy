Assumptions and limits

Income tax: personal allowance \u00a312,570 tapering by \u00a31 for every \u00a32 above \u00a3100,000; basic rate 20% to \u00a350,270 (a \u00a337,700 band above the allowance, so the 40% starting point falls as the allowance tapers); higher rate 40% to \u00a3125,140; additional rate 45% above. National Insurance: 8% between the \u00a312,570 primary threshold and \u00a350,270, then 2%. Student loan thresholds: Plan 1 \u00a326,900, Plan 2 \u00a329,385, Plan 4 \u00a333,795, Plan 5 \u00a325,000, Postgraduate \u00a321,000, all at 9% (Postgraduate 6%). England/Wales rules only — Scottish bands differ. Indicative, not tax advice.
