2026/27 UK Bank Holiday calendar quirk, council bin shifts & Day-4 turkey rule

Why Monday 28 December 2026 is a Bank Holiday: In 2026, Christmas Day falls on Friday 25 December and Boxing Day falls on Saturday 26 December. Under UK royal proclamation banking rules, Saturday's Boxing Day holiday rolls forward to Monday 28 December 2026 as an official substitute bank holiday across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland! Tuesday 29, Wednesday 30 and Thursday 31 December 2026 are normal non-bank-holiday weekdays before Friday 1 January 2027 (New Year's Day Bank Holiday).

Leftover turkey & council bins: Food Standards Agency (FSA) guidance recommends finishing or freezing cooked Christmas Day turkey within 48 hours (by Sunday 27 Dec evening, or Day 3–4 absolute max at ≤5 °C). Because Monday 28 Dec 2026 is a bank holiday, many UK councils push w/c 28 Dec collections forward by +1 day (e.g., Tuesday moves to Wednesday) — always double-check your council postcode lookup before leaving cardboard out in the rain!
