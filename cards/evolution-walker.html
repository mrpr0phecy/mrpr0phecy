How Evolution Works

Natural Selection

Creatures that walk farther, eat more, and survive events pass their genes to the next generation.

Neural Networks

Each creature has a brain that controls its leg timing, turning, and jumping based on terrain sensors.

Genetic Mutation

Random mutations introduce new body shapes, leg counts, armor, tails, and behavioral traits.

Environmental Pressure

Meteor showers, floods, predators, and earthquakes force creatures to adapt or perish.

Tips for Better Evolution

  • Higher mutation rates increase diversity but may break good designs
  • More creatures = faster evolution but lower FPS
  • Challenging terrain + events drives rapid adaptation
  • Try "extreme" events for the most dramatic evolution
  • Food system forces creatures to balance walking vs eating
  • Click "Trigger Event" to manually unleash chaos
  • Achievements track your most impressive evolutionary runs
