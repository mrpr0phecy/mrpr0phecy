🪣 Rainwater Harvesting & Tank Simulator

Everyone sizes a water butt by guessing. This runs a day-by-day simulation of a whole year — rain falling on your roof, water drawn off, the tank filling, overflowing and running dry — then sweeps every tank size from 100 to 20,000 litres to show you exactly where extra capacity stops buying you anything.
