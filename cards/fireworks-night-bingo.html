🎇 Fireworks Night Bingo

Play a simple fireworks spotting game with children and friends while watching a display.

Watch from the designated spectator area. Tick what you see; colours and shapes vary, so there is no need to finish the list.

Keep children supervised. Never pick up fireworks or debris; participation is optional and some displays may not show every item.
