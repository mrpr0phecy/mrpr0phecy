Surplus Materials Exchange

Give spare materials another useful life: match community projects to offered quantities, keep units consistent, and show leftovers and unmet needs before arranging collection.

A quantity-matching worksheet, not building, waste-handling or product-safety advice. Material names alone cannot establish suitability; inspection, dimensions and local rules still matter. Browser-only worksheet: no uploads, accounts or automatic saving. Use one agreed master copy; this is not a synchronised multi-user service. Export before closing and share only with consent.

    How this works and what it cannot do

    Exact case-insensitive material and unit matching. Requests are filled in input order and may draw from multiple donors. No unit conversions, transport routing, dimensions, condition or safety certification are inferred. Quantities are proposals only; confirm measurements and suitability before reuse.

    Agree participation, decision rules and responsibilities locally. These tools do not guarantee prosperity, check legal compliance or replace essential public services. No government funding or administration is assumed.
