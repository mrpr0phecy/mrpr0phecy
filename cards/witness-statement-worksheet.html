🗣 Neutral Witness Statement Worksheet

Capture a witness’s account in their own words, then record context separately. Use open prompts, interview witnesses individually, and avoid suggesting a sound, figure, or cause.

NON-LEADING · LOCAL-ONLY WORKSHEET
