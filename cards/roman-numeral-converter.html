The rules
• Additive — VI = 5 + 1 = 6.
• Subtractive — a smaller symbol before a larger one is subtracted: IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40, XC = 90, CD = 400, CM = 900.
• Never repeat a symbol more than 3 times — that is why 4 is IV, not IIII.
• No zero, and standard numerals run 1 (I) to 3999 (MMMCMXCIX).

Fun fact: Roman numerals were used for practical counting, not just dates — clock faces still use them today (usually with IIII, a traditional exception!).
