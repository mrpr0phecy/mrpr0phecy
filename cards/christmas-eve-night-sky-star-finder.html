On Christmas Eve 2026, the Moon is 15.4 days into its synodic cycle (99.5% illuminated Full Cold Moon), brightening winter rooftops all night. Historically, astronomers studying the "Star of Bethlehem" point to the rare 7 BC triple conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn in Pisces (discovered by Johannes Kepler) and the ultra-close 2 BC pairing of Jupiter and Venus in Leo—two steady, non-twinkling "wandering stars" you can compare tonight as Saturn sets in the west and blazing Jupiter rises in the east!
