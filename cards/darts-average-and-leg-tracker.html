🎯 Darts 501 Average & Leg Tracker

Log each visit of three darts and watch your three-dart average climb. Handles the bust rules for you — scoring more than you have left, or leaving yourself on 1 — and tracks legs won across a session.

Averages explained

Three-dart average = points scored ÷ darts thrown × 3 — the standard measure (the pros sit above 90, a good pub player around 60). Points per dart is the same total over every dart thrown. In proper 501 you must finish on a double or the bull; this tracker counts a leg when you reach exactly zero, so keep the double in mind.
