Steps to file:
Small Claims (UK)
• For claims up to £10,000 in England & Wales
• No lawyer needed
• Simple online process
• Bank usually settles before court

Court Fees
Up to £300£35
£300–£500£50

£5,000–£10,000£455

Before You File
1. Send a Letter Before Action (14 days)
2. Bank has 14 days to respond
3. If no resolution → file online
4. gov.uk/make-money-claim

What You Can Claim
• The disputed amount
• Court fees
• Interest (8% per year)
• Reasonable travel costs
• Loss of earnings (if applicable)
