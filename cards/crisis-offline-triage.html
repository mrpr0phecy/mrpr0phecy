🆘 Crisis Triage Guide

100% offline emergency triage protocols, acoustic CPR rhythm metronome, water survival calculator, and optical SOS distress beacon. Operates completely without internet during grid blackouts and natural disasters. ⚠️ In severe emergencies with cell signal, dial 999 / 911 immediately.

American Heart Assoc. CPR Standard
Continuous chest compressions at 100-120 BPM · 5-6 cm depth
1. Check Responsiveness: Shout & tap shoulders.
2. Call for Help: Designate someone specifically to dial emergency.
3. Hand Placement: Heel of one hand in center of chest, other hand interlaced on top.
4. Push Hard & Fast: Allow full chest recoil between compressions.
🛑 Life-Threatening Hemorrhage Protocol
1. Direct Unrelenting Pressure: Place clean gauze/cloth over wound and lean your entire upper-body weight directly onto the bleed.

2. Pack the Wound: If deep bleeding in junctional zones (groin, armpit, neck), pack cloth deeply into the wound cavity and maintain hard direct pressure for at least 3 minutes.

3. Tourniquet (Limbs Only): Place 2-3 inches above bleeding site (never over a joint). Wind windlass rod until bright arterial bleeding completely ceases and distal pulse vanishes. Lock rod and record exact time applied on patient's forehead (e.g., "TK 14:32").
🗣️ Airway Obstruction / Heimlich Maneuver
Can they cough or speak? If yes, encourage coughing. DO NOT slap their back while coughing.

If unable to breathe or silent:
• 5 Back Blows: Lean patient forward; deliver 5 sharp blows between shoulder blades with heel of hand.
• 5 Abdominal Thrusts: Stand behind victim, place fist thumb-side in just above navel, clasp other hand, pull inward and upward sharply.
• Unconscious: Lower gently to floor and begin CPR compressions immediately.
