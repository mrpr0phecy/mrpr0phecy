    Physics Notes & Related Winter Tools

    Feels-like temperature uses the JAG/TI wind-chill index (Twc = 13.12 + 0.6215·T − 11.37·V0.16 + 0.3965·T·V0.16 for T ≤ 10 °C and V > 4.8 km/h). Stroller passengers generate ~1.0 MET vs 2.8 MET for walkers, feeling roughly 3 °C colder. Below 4 °C, lithium-ion electrolyte viscosity rises sharply, causing temporary voltage sag under camera/4K video bursts unless kept inside an inner coat pocket.
