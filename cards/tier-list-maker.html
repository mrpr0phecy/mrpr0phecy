How to use it, what the link contains, and where your list is kept

Moving items. With a mouse, drag a tile to any tier and drop it where it should sit; a cyan bar shows the exact spot. On a touchscreen, press and hold a tile for a moment, then drag — a quick swipe still scrolls the page. You can also tap a tile to select it and then tap a tier's label (or use the Move to buttons). From the keyboard, Tab to a tile and press Enter to select it; the up and down arrows move it between tiers, left and right reorder it, Delete removes it and Escape lets go.

Tiers. Press a tier's coloured label to rename it, recolour it, move it up or down, empty it or delete it — the tiles in a deleted tier go back to Unranked. The letters are only a convention: “S” above “A” is usually traced to Japanese grading and video-game ranks, where S stands for something like “special” or “superior”. Rename the tiers to “Love / Like / Fine / Nope” if that suits the list better.

Photos. Photos are cropped to a square and shrunk to 240 pixels on your device before they are added, which keeps the board fast and the saved copy small. They never leave the browser.

The image. Download PNG draws the title and every tier at twice screen resolution, so it stays sharp when a social network compresses it. Unranked tiles are left out. The small credit in the corner is optional — untick the box and it is gone.

The share link. There is no server behind this tool: a share link carries the whole list inside the address itself — the title, tier names, colours and every text tile. Photos are too big to fit in a link and are left out. Anyone who opens the link is asked before it replaces their own board, so sending a friend your ranking never wipes theirs.

Saving. The board is saved in this browser's local storage as you work, so it is still here next time. Clearing site data clears it. There is no account, and nothing is sent anywhere.
