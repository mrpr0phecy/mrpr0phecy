Community Apprenticeship Planner

Turn volunteered teaching hours into small, realistic learning cohorts. Work out who can attend a complete course, how many places remain unmet, and the mentor time each skill requires.

Use aliases and consent-based sign-ups. Teaching capacity is not proof of competence, readiness for work or permission to practise regulated trades. All course requirements are entered by the organiser. Browser-only worksheet: no uploads, accounts or automatic saving. Use one agreed master copy; this is not a synchronised multi-user service. Export before closing and share only with consent.

One line: mentor alias | skill | total teaching hours | maximum cohort size

One line: unique skill | teaching hours per cohort

One line: unique learner alias | skill | available learning hours

Community proposal

    How this works and what it cannot do

    Mentors are processed in listed order. A full cohort consumes the course hours once from the mentor; each learner must individually have those hours available. Learners are filled in listed order, without duplicate placement. Matching is exact by skill, not availability dates. This is a capacity worksheet, not a certified qualification or an employment offer.

    Agree participation, decision rules and responsibilities locally. These tools do not guarantee prosperity, check legal compliance or replace essential public services. No government funding or administration is assumed.
