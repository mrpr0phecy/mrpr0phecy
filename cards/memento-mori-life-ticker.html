⏳ Memento Mori Life Ticker

Your life, visualised as a grid of weeks — then converted into how many summers, books and Sunday roasts you have left. Not to scare you. To make the next one count.

🪦 Life expectancy80 yrs
Methodology (gentle)

One square = one week, 52 squares = one year. Filled squares are already spent; empty ones are still on the board. The “how many more” list is just arithmetic — the point is that big numbers become small when you count them in experiences, which is exactly why this tool exists. No data leaves your browser.
