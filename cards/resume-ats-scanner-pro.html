How the score is calculated (and its limits)

Hard skills are extracted from the job ad by matching a 180-term dictionary (languages, frameworks, clouds, tools, certifications) plus capitalized bigrams/trigrams. ATS score = 35% keyword coverage · 20% hard-skill coverage · 15% formatting · 15% length/readability · 15% action verbs & quantifiable impact. Formatting traps checked: columns/tables (detected via tab runs & “|”), images, headers/footers, non-standard headings, PDF-only content, contact info completeness. This is heuristic, not a real Workday/Taleo engine — use it to close obvious gaps before a human reader.
