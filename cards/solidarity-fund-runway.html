Solidarity Fund Runway

Test a member-funded support pot before making promises. Model regular contributions, agreed grants and running costs, add a one-off disruption, and see when the fund crosses its reserve floor.

A hypothetical cash-flow worksheet, not insurance, investment advice or a benefit entitlement. Collection and support assumptions can fail. Get local advice on fund governance, safeguarding funds and any regulated activity. Last reviewed: 2026-10-05. Browser-only worksheet: no uploads, accounts or automatic saving. Use one agreed master copy; this is not a synchronised multi-user service. Export before closing and share only with consent.

    How this works and what it cannot do

    End-of-month balance = previous balance + expected collected contributions − support − costs − any one-off support. Money is rounded to cents. Collection rate is an assumption, not a default probability. A negative balance is an unmet funding need, not authorised borrowing. Reserve tests are at month end; within-month timing, inflation, tax and interest are excluded.

    Agree participation, decision rules and responsibilities locally. These tools do not guarantee prosperity, check legal compliance or replace essential public services. No government funding or administration is assumed.
