Exhibit B · Material comparison

Why materials matter

Thermal conductivity (W/m·K) measures how easily heat passes through a material. Copper is 400 — it's basically a heat highway. Brick is 0.6 — slow but steady. Mineral wool is 0.035 — heat barely gets through. That's a 10,000× difference between copper and insulation. Your wall is a carefully chosen stack of these barriers.
