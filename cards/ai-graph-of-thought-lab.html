🕸️ Graph-of-Thought Lab — Aggregation, Transformation, GoT Reasoning (Besta 2023/2024)

Launcher for the standalone AI: thoughts as a graph — merge, refine and loop back over candidate ideas with per-node scoring, instead of one linear chain. Runs in your browser.

Graph-of-Thought in the standalone AI treats each thought as a node: related thoughts are clustered, merged and refined, weak ones pruned, and the surviving graph is drawn so you can see why a conclusion won.

Open in the standalone AI

Opens at: Reason mode → Graph-of-Thought · runs entirely in your browser · no account, no API key, nothing uploaded

What the standalone AI gives you for this

  • Cosine-clustered thought graph with merge and refine operations
  • Per-node scores shown on the rendered graph
  • Compare the graph against Tree-of-Thought and Chain-of-Thought on one click
