    How straight-wrap & 45° diagonal wrapping maths work

    Standard orthogonal cut formula

    To wrap around the box girth cleanly with a neat folded seam, one dimension must cover 2 × (W + H) + overlap (5 cm / 2 in). The perpendicular dimension covers the box length plus two end-flaps that meet past the middle: L + (1.35 × H) + margin. Because you can orient the box along or across the roll, the calculator tests both orientations and chooses whichever fits your roll width while unrolling the least paper.

    Why rotating the box 45° rescues a "too-small" piece of paper

    When a sheet is slightly too narrow for 2 × (W + H) straight on, placing the box at 45° turns the four triangular corners into overlapping envelope flaps that meet at the centre of the top face. For a rectangular box of dimensions L × W × H, the minimum square side required at 45° is (L + W) / √2 + √2 × H + overlap. Whenever H is modest compared to L and W, a narrow roll that fails straight-on can succeed diagonally!
