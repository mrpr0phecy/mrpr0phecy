How everyone in the group chat gets a fair, different 5×5 card

Deterministic name seeding (zero accounts, zero servers)

When you send this link to your family or Slack group, every person types their own name (with the same Card # and Trope Pack). A deterministic 32-bit hash shuffles the 28-item trope bank uniquely for each name — so Cousin Alex, Auntie Pam and Sam each play a genuinely different 5×5 card completely offline.

Winning lines

Stamp any 5 across a row, 5 down a column, or 5 corner-to-corner diagonally (the centre FREE square starts stamped) to trigger BINGO, or chase all 25 for a Full House.
