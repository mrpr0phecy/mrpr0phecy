Reverse percentage — find the original amount before a % change

Formula: change % = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. For a reverse percentage, divide by the multiplier: original = result ÷ (1 ± p/100). A change from 25 to 30 is a 20% increase — and from 30 back to 25 it's a 16.7% decrease, because the base differs.
