Sizes, posting order and how to get a seamless result

Carousels. Instagram shows every slide of a carousel at the same aspect ratio, so a panorama split into equal slices swipes as one continuous image. The 4:5 portrait slide (1080×1350) takes the most screen space in the feed and is the default; squares and 9:16 story slides are also offered. A carousel holds up to 20 slides on the current app, but the seamless effect works best at 2–5 — pick the count that gives the least cropping (the note under the preview tells you how much of the picture is kept). Slides are numbered left to right; upload them in that order and they stitch together.

Profile grids. Since early 2025 the profile grid shows posts as 3:4 rectangles, so the 3:4 tile (1080×1440) is the default; the classic 1:1 square and 4:5 are there for older-style feeds and other apps. The catch with grids is posting order: the profile shows the newest post first, at the top left, so the tile that belongs at the bottom right has to go up first and the top-left tile last. The tiles below are labelled with that order and the ZIP names them post-01 to post-09 in the order to upload, so you can work through them from the top. Every new post shifts the grid, so a 3-wide picture stays aligned only if you keep posting in multiples of three afterwards.

Grid-gap trim. The app draws a thin gap between grid tiles, so a picture cut into perfect slices looks stretched by that gap. Trimming 1–2% between tiles compensates: each tile loses a sliver along the shared edge, and the seams line up on screen. Carousels have no gap, so leave it at None for them.

Fill or fit. Fill scales the photo to cover the whole layout and crops the rest; the position sliders choose which part is kept. Fit keeps the entire photo and pads the sides with a colour — handy for logos or when nothing can be cut. Output is JPEG at quality 92 (Instagram recompresses anything you upload, so heavier files gain nothing) or PNG if you need lossless. The ZIP is built in the page from the same tiles, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
