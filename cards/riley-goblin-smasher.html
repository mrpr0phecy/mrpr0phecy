🧙 Riley: Goblin Smasher

A free, offline 3D arena battler in raw WebGL — no assets, no downloads. Riley the wizard smashes endless goblin waves with fireballs, dashes and double jumps.

🎮 Play Full Screen
Controls
W A S D / arrows — move   W ×2 — double jump   SPACE — fireball   SHIFT — dash-smash   P — pause   M — mute

Features
• Wave-based arena combat with 3 goblin breeds: Grunts, ranged Spitters and lunging Brutes
• Every 5th wave a GOBLIN KING drops in with a health bar, minion summons and an enrage phase
• 6 rotating themes rebuild the floating-island arena each wave
• Combo multiplier — every 20-chain grants a bonus heart
• Dash through goblins to smash them instantly
• Procedural SFX, particles, screen shake and score roll-up

Tips
• Chain kills fast — the combo timer drains in seconds
• Spitters out-range you: close in or duck behind cover, then dash
• Watch the lunge telegraph on Brutes and the King, then sidestep
• Don't fall into the void around the arena — it hurts!
