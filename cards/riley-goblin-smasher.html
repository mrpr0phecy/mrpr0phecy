🧙 Riley: Goblin Smasher

Procedural platformer with wave-based combat. Play as Riley the wizard, smash goblins with fireballs, dash through levels!

🎮 Play Full Screen
Controls
A/D or ←/→ — Move   W or ↑ — Jump   SPACE — Fireball   SHIFT — Dash

Features
• 3 goblin types: Standard, Berserker (chases you), Brute (3 HP tank)
• Procedural level generation — every wave is different
• 7 themed environments (Night Forest → Void Realm)
• Combo system — chain kills for bonus points
• Dash ability for quick escapes
• Particle effects and screen shake

Tips
• Berserkers chase you — use platforms to outmaneuver them
• Brutes take 3 hits — kite them while shooting
• Chain kills quickly for combo multipliers
• Use SHIFT dash to dodge goblin charges
