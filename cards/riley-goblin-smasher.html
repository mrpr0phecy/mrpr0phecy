🧙 Riley Smashes the Zombies

Massively upgraded 2D arcane platformer! Play as Riley the Wizard, cast 4 switchable elemental spells, execute seismic ground slams, unlock permanent perk upgrades, and smash hordes of mutant zombies & colossal bosses!

Controls & Abilities
A/D or ←/→ — Move   W or ↑ — Jump   S or ↓ — Seismic Ground Slam
SPACE — Cast Active Spell   SHIFT — Invulnerable Dash   1 / 2 / 3 / 4 — Switch Spells (Fire, Lightning, Frost, Meteor)
R — Unleash Apocalypse Meteor Smash when Arcane Rage meter is full!
Touch Controls — Full on-screen buttons available on mobile and tablet devices.

Massive Upgrades & Features
• 4 Elemental Spells: Explosive Fireball, Chain Lightning, Freezing Frost Nova, and Heavy Void Meteor
• Seismic Ground Slam: Downward aerial strike that creates a shockwave crushing surrounding zombies
• 7 Zombie & Monster Archetypes: Walkers, Runners, Acid Spitters, Boomers, Armored Goliaths, Necromancers
• Colossal Multi-Stage Bosses: Face the Abomination Titan and Necrolord every 5 waves
• Wave Upgrade Emporium: Spend earned gold on Split Shot, Infernal Core, Titan Slam, Frostbite, and Shield perks
• Procedural Dynamic Biomes: Haunted Graveyard, Toxic Mire, Crimson Citadel, Cosmic Sanctum, Frozen Necropolis
• Integrated Procedural Web Audio: Standalone retro sound effects and synthesizer
• Python / Pygame Standalone Edition: Full executable script available at riley_smashes_zombies.py
