    Equations & physical constants used in the North Pole lab

    Why Santa gets 31 hours instead of 24

    Flying westward from the International Date Line (Kiribati, UTC+14) toward Baker Island (UTC−12) while chasing local midnight across Earth's rotation stretches Christmas Eve night-time to roughly 31–32 hours.

    Equations

    Total route distance per sleigh is D = (N × d_hop) / S, giving mean speed v = D / T. Constants: speed of sound at sea level 343 m/s, speed of light c = 299,792.458 km/s, standard gravity g₀ = 9.80665 m/s², Eiffel Tower mass 10,100 metric tonnes. When β = v / c < 1, Lorentz factor is γ = 1 / √(1 − β²) and relativistic kinetic energy is (γ − 1) m c² (evaluated with m c² · β²/2 series expansion at small β to prevent floating-point cancellation).
