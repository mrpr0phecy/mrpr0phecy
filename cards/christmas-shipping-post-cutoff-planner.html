How December postal cutoffs, volumetric billing (L × W × H ÷ 5000) & Friday Dec 25 work

2026 calendar note (Christmas Day = Friday 25 Dec 2026): Because Christmas Eve 2026 falls on Thursday 24 December, typical UK domestic planning cutoffs cluster around 18 December for Economy / 2nd Class, 20–21 December for 1st Class / Tracked 48, 21–22 December for Tracked 24, and 23 December for Special Delivery Guaranteed. International air parcels require customs clearance buffer, placing Australia/NZ in the first week of December and USA/EU between 9–14 December.

Volumetric weight surprise: Light but bulky Christmas gifts (plush toys, winter coats, gift hampers) are billed by couriers on whichever is heavier: scale dead weight or dimensional weight (L × W × H in cm) ÷ 5000. Trimming 4 cm of empty air out of an oversized outer carton often drops an international parcel down an entire £12–£18 tariff band!

Typical planning guide only — always verify live annual cutoff dates and weather advisories directly with Royal Mail, USPS or your chosen courier before posting. Last reviewed: 2026-11-12.
