The method
1. Divide — how many times does the divisor go into the current part?
2. Multiply — write the product under the part.
3. Subtract — the difference is the remainder so far.
4. Bring down — the next digit joins the remainder.
Repeat until no digits remain. Check: quotient × divisor + remainder = dividend.

Note: if the dividend is not divisible, the remainder is the amount left over — it must always be smaller than the divisor.
