FATAL TOXICITY HAZARDS: 1. NEVER add red food dye! Artificial Red #40 causes irreversible avian renal/hepatic failure. The red plastic on your feeder is all that is needed.
2. NEVER use honey, raw turbinado, or brown sugar! Honey breeds deadly bacterial fungus that causes the hummingbird's tongue to swell, resulting in slow starvation. Use strictly refined white table sugar.
