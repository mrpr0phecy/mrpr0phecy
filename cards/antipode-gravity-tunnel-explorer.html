🕳️ Antipode & Gravity Tunnel Explorer

Find the exact point on the far side of the planet from anywhere, then drill a straight frictionless tunnel to any second place and drop a capsule down it. The fall time is integrated through the real PREM density model of the Earth's interior — not the textbook uniform sphere — so you get the honest 38 minutes, the peak speed, the depth reached, and the pressure and temperature the tunnel would have to survive.
