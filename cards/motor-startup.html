⚠️ Safety & Installation Guidelines
⚡ Electrical Safety
  • Always de-energize before installation
  • Use proper PPE (gloves, goggles)
  • Follow lockout-tagout procedures
  • Verify zero voltage before touching
🔧 Installation
  • Proper cable sizing and termination
  • Correct motor rotation direction
  • Adequate ventilation and cooling
  • Secure mounting and alignment
📋 Testing & Commissioning
  • Insulation resistance test (>1 MΩ)
  • Check phase sequence and balance
  • Verify protection device settings
  • Monitor starting current and time

📚 Motor Calculation Formulas & Standards
⚡ Running Current Formula
3-phase: I = P / (√3 × V × PF × η)
1-phase: I = P / (V × PF × η)
Where:
I = Current (A)
P = Power (W)
V = Voltage (V)
PF = Power Factor
η = Efficiency
🚀 Starting Current Multipliers
• DOL: 6–8× FLC
• Star-Delta: 2–3× FLC
• Soft Starter: 1.5–3× FLC
• VFD: 1–1.5× FLC
• Autotransformer: 2–4× FLC
Actual values vary by motor design
🔌 Voltage Drop Formula
Vdrop = √3 × I × L × (R cosφ + X sinφ)
Where:
I = Current (A)
L = Cable length (m)
R = Resistance (Ω/m)
X = Reactance (Ω/m)
Acceptable drop: <3% for motors
🏭 IEC/NEC Standards
• IEC 60034: Rotating electrical machines
• IEC 60947: Low-voltage switchgear
• NEC Article 430: Motors & controllers
• BS 7671: UK wiring regulations
Always follow local codes and standards
