  • Symbols are added left to right (VI = 6) unless a smaller value precedes a larger one, in which case it's subtracted (IV = 4).
  • Only I, X and C can be subtracted, and only from the next two higher symbols (IV, IX, XL, XC, CD, CM).
  • No symbol repeats more than three times in a row — 4 is IV, not IIII (clock faces are the famous exception).
  • A bar over a numeral multiplies it by 1,000 (V̅ = 5,000). Romans had no zero; "nulla" was used in medieval texts.
