How compression works & privacy

Images are decoded to bitmap in this tab, optionally resized with high-quality bicubic (imageSmoothingQuality='high'), then re-encoded via canvas.toDataURL(mime, quality). Quality affects JPEG/WebP only; PNG ignores it but still benefits from resizing and EXIF stripping. EXIF stripping happens automatically because canvas re-encode drops all metadata — GPS, camera model, etc. never leaves your device. Watermark is painted last via 2D context. Close the tab and everything is gone.
