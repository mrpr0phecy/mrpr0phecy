D + E explained

The final score is the D-score (difficulty of the skills and connections, open-ended) plus the E-score (execution), which starts at 10 and loses points for deductions. Women's artistic scores often land around 13–15 on floor, men's 14–16. Deduction values vary by apparatus and code of points — these are typical ballpark figures.
