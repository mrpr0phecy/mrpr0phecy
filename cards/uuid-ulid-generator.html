🆔 UUID & ULID Generator

Generate cryptographically random UUID v4, time-sortable UUID v7, ULIDs, nano IDs and short slugs in bulk. Everything is produced locally with your browser's crypto engine — nothing is requested from, or sent to, a server.

Which one should I use?
  • v4 — the safe default. 122 random bits, no ordering, no information leak.
  • v7 / ULID — sortable by creation time, so they index far better as database primary keys.
  • Nano ID — shorter, URL-safe, good for public-facing links.
  • Short slug — 8 characters only: fine for share codes, not for anything that must never collide.
