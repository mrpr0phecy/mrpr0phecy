🔬 Astronomy model (real mechanics)

Each planet follows its real orbit: the radius is r = a(1−e²)/(1+e·cos θ), and θ comes from solving Kepler's equation M = E − e·sin E at every frame (Newton's method), so orbital speed is genuinely faster at perihelion. Distances, periods, eccentricities and inclinations are the real values; only planet sizes are exaggerated to stay visible (switch "True scale" and they shrink to dots — which is exactly what the real solar system looks like from above). Inclinations are exaggerated ×5 so the 3D weaving is visible. Asteroid belt is procedural at 2.2–3.2 au.
