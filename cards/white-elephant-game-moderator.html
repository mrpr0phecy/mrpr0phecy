    Official White Elephant / Yankee Swap rules enforced by this moderator

    How turns and chain steals work

    Player #1 opens the first wrapped gift. Starting with Player #2, each player may either unwrap a fresh gift from the pile (which advances to the next numbered player) or steal any unlocked open gift. Whoever just had their gift stolen immediately takes a replacement turn — either unwrapping a new gift or stealing a different open gift.

    Why immediate steal-backs & freeze caps matter

    Without two guardrails, two stubborn cousins can pass the same espresso maker back and forth until New Year's Day. Blocking immediate steal-backs means Gift A cannot be taken straight back on the very next sub-turn, and freezing a gift after 2–3 steals locks it permanently with its lucky owner.

    Player #1's final advantage

    Because Player #1 had zero gifts to steal on the opening move, traditional rules grant Player #1 one final chance once the last wrapped box is opened: keep their current gift and end the game, or swap for any non-frozen gift on the table.
