📖 Plain Language Decoder

Paste a letter, contract clause, medical note or official notice and get it back in ordinary English, with the obligations and deadlines pulled out separately. Runs entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded, which matters when the text contains your personal details.

Paste some text and press Decode it.
What this does not do

This is a reading aid, not legal or medical advice. It cannot know your circumstances, and it will not interpret what a clause means for your particular case. If money, housing, health or your immigration status depends on the document, take the plain-English version to Citizens Advice, a law centre, your GP or a specialist adviser — it will save them time and get you a better answer faster.

Being handed something you cannot read is one of the most common ways people lose rights they actually have. Understanding the words is usually the first step to keeping them.
