    Food Science Notes & Related Kitchen Planners

    Adding ½ teaspoon (~2.5 g) of sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3) per litre of parboil water raises pH to ~8.8, accelerating pectin β-elimination in the outer 2 mm of potato tissue. Tossing drained chunks creates an amylose-rich surface slurry that dehydrates in hot fat above 190 °C into a honeycombed Maillard glass crust—provided each chunk gets at least 32 cm2 of tray area so escaping steam vents upward instead of softening neighbours.
