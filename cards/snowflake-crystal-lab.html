🔬 Modelling notes (real physics used)

Habit follows the Nakaya / Kobayashi diagram: plates near −2°C and −12…−16°C, needles at −5°C, hollow columns at −8°C and −22…−25°C; higher supersaturation drives branching from plates into sectored plates then dendrites. Growth is diffusion-limited: the crystal consumes local water vapour, so the effective supersaturation falls and growth stalls — the same depletion that creates the characteristic fern shape. Six-fold symmetry arranges the arms at 60°.
