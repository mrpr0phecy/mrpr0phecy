Tally a driver's season under the current 2025 points system — 25 down to 1 for the top ten, 8 down to 1 for a sprint — and see the running total, points per round, and how it compares with a rival.

The 2025 points system

Grand Prix: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1 for the top ten (11th or lower scores nothing). Sprint: 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 for the top eight. The fastest-lap bonus point was removed for 2025, so it is not counted here.
