The SGLT-1 Cotransport Secret: In the small intestine, sodium cannot be efficiently absorbed against a concentration gradient alone. The Sodium-Glucose Luminal Transporter (SGLT-1) requires 1 molecule of glucose to pull 2 sodium ions and over 200 water molecules across into the bloodstream. Pure water passes right through; real rehydration requires trace glucose and salt together!
