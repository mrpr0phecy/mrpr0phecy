🏏 Cricket Net Run Rate Calculator

The tie-breaker that decides tournaments. Add each match's runs and overs for and against, and this works out your aggregate Net Run Rate — applying the rule that a team bowled out is counted as facing its full overs.

How NRR is worked out

Net Run Rate = (total runs scored ÷ total overs faced) − (total runs conceded ÷ total overs bowled). Overs faced excludes rain-shortened overs, but if a side is all out they are deemed to have faced the full quota — tick “all out” and the full overs are used for that innings. Balls are the extra deliveries after the completed overs (0–5).
