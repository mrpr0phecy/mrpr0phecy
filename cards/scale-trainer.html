📐 Scale Formulas
  • Major: W–W–H–W–W–W–H (whole/half steps)
  • Natural minor: W–H–W–W–H–W–W
  • Pentatonic: 5 notes from the major scale (1,2,3,5,6)
  • Blues: Pentatonic + flat 5th (1,b3,4,b5,5,b7)
