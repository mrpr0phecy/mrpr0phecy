Focus:

Tips: choosing a layout, sizes that suit each platform, and getting sharp results

Layouts. The list shows every layout that fits the number of photos you have added, plus an automatic grid for any count. Photos fill the slots in tray order; tap a photo in the collage to select it, then tap another to swap the two. With Fill each slot the photo is cropped to the slot’s shape — use the focus arrows to choose which part is kept, for instance Top for a portrait so heads are not cut off. Show whole photo never crops; the background colour fills the spare space instead.

Sizes. Square 2048 is a safe all-rounder for Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook, and it is the size most apps downscale to anyway. Portrait 4:5 gives the biggest possible Instagram feed post; Story 9:16 fits stories, reels covers and phone lock screens. The A4 options render at 300 dots per inch (2480 × 3508 pixels) for photo-quality prints; a collage made at that size prints cleanly on a home printer or at a print shop.

Sharpness. Photos are kept at up to 3000 pixels on their longest side, so even a slot that takes up the whole canvas stays crisp. Save as PNG for the sharpest result and exact colours; choose JPEG when the file needs to be small — it is about a fifth of the size, which matters for a 300 dpi A4 page. Copy image puts a PNG on the clipboard for pasting straight into a message or document (in browsers that allow it).

Privacy. Nothing is uploaded; the photos stay in this tab and disappear when you close it. Only your layout settings are remembered in this browser.
