Challenge incorrect entries on your credit report — generate dispute letters for credit agencies and banks

Your Rights
Under UK GDPR and the Consumer Credit Act, credit agencies must ensure data is accurate. You have the right to:
• Dispute any inaccurate entry
• Have errors corrected within 28 days
• Add a notice of correction to your file
• Complain to the ICO if they refuse

Free Credit Reports
• Experian: free via MoneySavingExpert
• Equifax: free via ClearScore
• TransUnion: free via Credit Karma

Dispute Process
1. Get your free credit report
2. Identify incorrect entries
3. Write to the credit agency
4. Agency investigates (28 days)
5. If rejected → complain to ICO
6. Also dispute directly with the bank

Common Errors
• Payments marked late when they weren't
• Accounts that aren't yours
• Settled debts shown as active
• Wrong credit limits
• Outdated defaults (>6 years)
