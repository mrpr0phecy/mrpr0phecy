🔥 Wildfire Spread Simulator

Click to drop a spark. Fire then spreads cell-by-cell using a Rothermel-style rate of spread: fuel, dead-fuel moisture, wind and slope. Watch the head fire run with the wind, the flanking fire crawl, and lakes stop the front.

Click the map to ignite. Head-fire ROS and Byram intensity update live.
The physics

Each cell is fuel, rock or water. A burning cell ignites a neighbour with probability proportional to remaining fuel × (1 − moisture) × a wind alignment term (cos θ of the spread vector vs the wind) × a slope term (fires run uphill). Residence time scales with fuel load. Rate of spread is the head-fire distance per simulated minute; Byram intensity I = H·w·R (heat yield × consumed fuel × ROS) is what firefighters use to decide whether a front is attackable.
