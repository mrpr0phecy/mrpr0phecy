📈 CAGR — Compound Annual Growth

CAGR = (end ÷ start)1/years − 1. The smoothed yearly rate that takes a starting value to an ending value. It ignores deposits, fees and the bumpy path in between — a sketch, not a forecast.

Edit start, end and years to get CAGR — or set start, years and CAGR % to project an ending value.
