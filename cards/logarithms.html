Rules and identities

logb(x) answers "b to what power gives x?", so it is only defined for x > 0 and b > 0, b \u2260 1. Change of base: logb(x) = ln(x) / ln(b). Product: log(xy) = log x + log y. Quotient: log(x/y) = log x \u2212 log y. Power: log(x\u207f) = n\u00b7log x. Doubling time at growth rate r is ln(2)/ln(1+r), which is what the Rule of 72 approximates.
