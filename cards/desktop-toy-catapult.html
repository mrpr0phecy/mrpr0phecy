🏹 Catapult Simulator

Build a medieval trebuchet, load projectiles, set the counterweight, and launch!

How it works
A trebuchet uses a counterweight to swing the throwing arm. The heavier the weight and the better the angle, the farther the projectile flies. Wind and air resistance add realism.

Controls
• Angle — release angle of the arm
• Weight — counterweight mass
• Launch — fire the trebuchet
• Reset — reload and reset position
