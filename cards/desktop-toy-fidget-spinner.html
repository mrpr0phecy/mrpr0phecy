🌀 Fidget Spinner

Spin a fidget spinner with realistic angular momentum — flick to spin, hold to accelerate, watch it slow down

How it works
The spinner has angular momentum — it spins freely with very low friction. Each arm has weighted tips that create a satisfying gyroscopic effect. The faster it spins, the more stable it feels.

Controls
• Click & drag — spin by dragging around center
• Flick — give it a random spin
• Stop — brake the spinner
• Friction — how fast it slows down
• Arms — number of spinner arms
