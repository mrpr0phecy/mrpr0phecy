Sonic Games

From the 1991 original to the open-zone era — a fan's tour of the series.

Sonic the Hedgehog

1991 · Mega Drive / Genesis

The game that started it all. Spin-dash through Green Hill, free the animals and put a stop to Dr. Robotnik's machine plans.

Sonic CD

1993 · Sega CD

Time travel, a race against Metal Sonic and one of the most celebrated soundtracks of the 16-bit era — Sonic's strangest, fondest detour.

Sonic Adventure 2

2001 · Dreamcast

Hero and Dark campaigns, the debut of Shadow and Rouge, and the Chao garden that kept players busy for years.

Sonic Frontiers

2022

Sonic's first open-zone adventure — explore the Starfall Islands, solve puzzles and face giant guardians in sprawling landscapes.

Sonic Mania

2017

A love letter to classic Sonic, built by fans and blessed by SEGA — remixed zones, new stages and a pixel-perfect sense of speed.

Sonic Generations

2011

Classic and modern Sonic race side by side through two eras of levels. Re-released in 2024 with a new Shadow campaign.

Sonic Characters

The regulars of the series, in one fan's own words.

Sonic

Main Protagonist

The fastest thing alive. Blue blur, golden rings and an unshakeable sense of right.

Tails

Best Friend

Miles "Tails" Prower — two-tailed genius inventor, pilot and Sonic's most loyal sidekick.

Knuckles

Guardian

The echidna guardian of the Master Emerald on Angel Island — and the last of his kind.

Dr. Eggman

Main Antagonist

Dr. Ivo Robotnik. Genius inventor, world-conquering ambitions, chronically bad luck with hedgehogs.

Amy Rose

Adventurer

Hammer-wielding adventurer with a big heart — always ready to help, whatever the adventure.

Shadow

Ultimate Life Form

Created on the Space Colony ARK fifty years ago. Edge to spare, chaos control at his fingertips.
