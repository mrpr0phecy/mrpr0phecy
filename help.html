Help & Contact

Most questions are answered below instantly. If yours isn't, email me — it's one person in Luton, and a direct message gets a real reply.

Using the tools

Is it really free? What's the catch?

Genuinely free. All 562 tools, no sign-up, no account, no trial, no paywall, no premium tier. There is no catch and no plan to add one. The site is hosted free on GitHub Pages, so it costs almost nothing to run. Donations are welcome but change nothing about what you can access.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login anywhere on the site, so there is nothing to register for and no password to forget.

Can I use these tools for commercial or client work?

Yes. Use them for anything lawful, personal or commercial, with no licence fee and no attribution required. The site's code is MIT licensed — see the IP section. The MrProphecy music and artwork are not covered by that licence.

Can I embed a tool on my own website?

Yes. Open any tool and press the Embed button in its footer to copy an iframe snippet you can paste straight into your page.

Do the tools work offline?

Once a tool has loaded it runs entirely in your browser, so it keeps working if your connection drops. The catalogue index itself needs one initial load to fetch the tool list.

A tool gave me a wrong answer, or something is broken

Please report it. Email hello@themostusefulsiteintheworld.com with the tool name, what you entered, what it returned and what you expected. Calculation errors are treated as urgent and usually fixed within a day or two.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes, and suggestions are where a lot of these came from. Email the address above with what the tool should do and what problem it solves for you. No promises on timing.

Privacy and safety

Do you track me, use cookies or run analytics?

Partly — here's the honest answer. The site loads Google Analytics, which sets third-party cookies and counts visits in aggregate. That's how I can tell which of the 562 tools people actually use and price sponsorship without guessing. It runs with IP anonymisation on and ad personalisation off, and there is no advertising network anywhere on the site.

What is never collected: anything you type into a tool. Those inputs are processed on your device and never transmitted. To opt out, use Google's opt-out add-on or any content blocker — the site works perfectly with analytics blocked. Full detail in the privacy section.

Where does the data I type into a tool go?

Nowhere. Every tool runs inside your browser. There is no server and no database behind this site, so your inputs are processed on your own device and never transmitted to anyone.

Some tools remember my entries. Where is that stored?

On your device, in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your machine and only this site in that browser can read it. Clear your browser's site data to erase it completely.

Can I trust a health, money or electrical calculation?

Treat every result as an estimate, not professional advice. Health tools are not medical advice, finance tools are not regulated financial advice, electrical tools are sizing aids that a qualified person must sign off, and document generators are drafts, not legal advice. Check anything that matters with a qualified professional — see the full disclaimer.
