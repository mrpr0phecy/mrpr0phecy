Manifesto
“We don’t decorate skin. We commit to it.”

A tattoo is the only art you can’t hang straight. It moves, stretches, ages, scars — and if it’s drawn wrong, it tells that lie forever. At NOIR we draw for the body in motion, not for a photo grid. Every stencil is placed, then re-drawn freehand until the flow belongs to its owner.

No walk-ins. No re-used flash. No photoshop smoothing before posting. You’ll see healed photos at the same size you’ll wear. If we wouldn’t wear it at 60, we won’t tattoo it at 26.

    Aftercare — In 4 Steps

    Leave second-skin on for 24h. If we used cling, remove after 3h, wash gently with fragrance-free soap, pat dry, no moisturiser yet. No gym, no soak, no sun.
    Wash 2× daily, thin layer of aftercare balm (we give you one). No picking scabs, no tight clothing rubbing. Sleep on clean sheets.
    Itch = healing. Keep moisturised lightly, keep out of sun and pools. No fake tan, no exfoliants on the tattoo.
    SPF 50 on the tattoo forever — sun is tattoos’ worst enemy. Message us a healed photo at 3 weeks; we’ll invite you for a free touch-up if needed.
    Increasing pain after day 3, spreading redness, hot swelling, pus, fever — contact us and your GP promptly. We’re not medical, but we’ll triage and support.
