🏷️ The Thing Namer

🏷️ The Thing Namer

Naming things is one of the two hard problems in computer science (along with cache invalidation and off-by-one errors). Point it at a thing and it will name it — band, pet, D&D character, startup, WiFi network, the lot.

Methodology (the naming engine)

Each type draws from its own bank of adjectives, nouns and suffixes and stitches them together with a shabby randomness. Names are generated on your device and not checked for trademark, availability, or dignity. Click a name to copy it.
