How pure WebAudio harmonic bell synthesis (f = 440 × 2^((m−69)/12)) works with zero MP3 files

Zero-file additive bell synthesis: Every note is synthesised live in your browser from its equal-temperament MIDI pitch m via f = 440 × 2^((m − 69)/12) Hz. To mimic Tchaikovsky's 1892 celesta and brass handbells without downloading a single kilobyte of audio, each strike layers a warm fundamental sine oscillator (1.0 × f) with an inharmonic bell overtone (2.0× or 2.756 × f) shaped by an exponential percussive decay envelope (0.008 s attack, 0.65 s shimmer release).

Visual contour accessibility: Even with sound muted on a train or in a sleeping household, seasoned music lovers can spot Joy to the World's unmistakable 8-note descending major scale (`C5 → B4 → A4 → G4 → F4 → E4 → D4 → C4`) or Jingle Bells' repeated `E4–E4–E4` rhythmic triplet directly on the gold piano-roll contour! All 12 melodies are 16th- to 19th-century public-domain compositions.
