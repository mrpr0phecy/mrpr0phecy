♿ Accessible Text Prep

♿ Accessible Text Prep

Paste something you need a specific person to understand — a letter from a council, instructions for an elderly relative, a form your colleague keeps skipping. This rewrites the presentation and the sentences for that reader, and scores it before and after so you can see whether it actually got easier.

Nothing is uploaded. All of this runs in your browser.
What each mode changes, and why

Easy Read splits every sentence at its conjunctions so there is one idea per line, replaces the common bureaucratic words, and bullets anything that is really a list. This follows the established Easy Read convention of short lines, plain words and generous space.

Dyslexic reader keeps your words but breaks long lines, avoids justified text (which creates rivers of whitespace), and adds space between paragraphs. Low vision strips everything decorative and prints at 18pt with high contrast. ESL expands contractions and flags idioms, which are the part of English that does not translate. Anxious reader moves the action to the top, because someone who is frightened cannot hold a long preamble.

The score is Flesch Reading Ease: higher is easier, 60–70 is plain English, below 30 is academic or legal.
