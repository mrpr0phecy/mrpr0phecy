Thermohaline Circulation Secret: For fresh water, density peaks at 3.98°C. But salt lowers the peak density temperature below the freezing point! Thus, cold salty polar seawater continually becomes denser as it chills, sinking to the abyss and driving the planetary oceanic conveyor belt.
