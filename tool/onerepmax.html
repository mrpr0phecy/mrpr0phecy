Which Formula Should You Use?

Epley Formula Best for: General Training
1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)
Most widely used and validated formula. Works well for 1-10 reps. Slightly overestimates for very high rep sets.
Brzycki Formula Best for: Conservative Estimates
1RM = Weight ÷ (1.0278 − 0.0278 × Reps)
Safer for beginners. More conservative estimate, especially with higher reps. Good for injury prevention.
Lombardi Formula Best for: Powerlifters
1RM = Weight × Reps^0.1
Better for powerlifters doing low-rep, high-intensity work. Tends to give higher estimates for experienced lifters.
⚠️ Safety First: These are estimates. Never test your actual 1RM without proper warm-up, equipment, and spotters. Progress gradually.
