🫧 Lava Lamp

🫧 Lava Lamp

Mesmerizing blob physics — watch wax rise, split, merge, and fall in a heated glass vessel

How it works
Wax blobs are heated from the bottom, become less dense, and rise. At the top they cool, become denser, and sink. Surface tension creates satisfying blob shapes that merge and split.

Controls
• Heat — more heat = faster rising
• Blob Count — number of wax blobs
• Wax Color — pick your lava color
• Click/tap the lamp to poke blobs
