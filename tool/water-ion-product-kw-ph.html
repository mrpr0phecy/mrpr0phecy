Biomedical Insight: At human body temperature (37°C), pure water has a pH of 6.81, not 7.00! Because auto-ionization is endothermic, heat drives dissociation forward. Arterial blood pH of 7.40 at 37°C is actually slightly alkaline relative to neutral water.
