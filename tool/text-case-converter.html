Case style cheat sheet

camelCase and PascalCase are common in code, snake_case works well for database fields, and kebab-case is readable in filenames. A URL slug removes punctuation and accents so it is portable across systems. Title Case keeps small words (a, the, of…) lowercase unless they lead.
