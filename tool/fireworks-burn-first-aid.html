🩹 Fireworks Burn First Aid

A step-by-step reminder for a heat burn while you get appropriate medical help.

Emergency: call 999 for serious injury, breathing trouble or if the person is unresponsive. For chemical or electrical burns seek urgent medical help. This is first-aid information, not a diagnosis. Last reviewed: 2026-11-03.

Reference: NHS: burns and scalds treatment.
