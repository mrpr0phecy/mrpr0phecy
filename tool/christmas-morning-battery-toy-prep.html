⚠️ Child Button-Cell Safety Alert (CAPT / NHS): Lithium coin batteries (CR2032 / LR44 inside novelty singing cards, light-up jumpers and flameless tea lights) can cause severe internal burns within 2 hours if swallowed. Check every battery compartment has a captive screw and store spare coin cells out of sight and reach.

Why Christmas Day console servers crawl — and why PH0 beats a kitchen knife

The Christmas Morning CDN Congestion Factor: Between 08:00 and 14:00 on 25 December, millions of brand-new consoles hit PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo update servers simultaneously, often cutting real-world download throughput by 25–40% below your router's nominal Mbps speed (`1 byte = 8 bits`, plus ~12% TCP/TLS & decompression overhead). Unbox the console secretly on Dec 23/24 evening, run firmware and game installs over Ethernet, then slide it neatly back into its cardboard sleeve so it boots straight into gameplay on Christmas morning!

The PH0 captive-screw trap: Under EN 62115 toy safety standards, every children's toy battery door uses a recessed miniature Phillips #0 or #1 screw. Standard DIY `#2` screwdrivers are too wide to reach down the plastic well, and butter knives strip the brass head in seconds — put a `#0` precision screwdriver and blunt-nosed wire cutters (for plastic packaging ties) in a mug beside the tree on Christmas Eve!
