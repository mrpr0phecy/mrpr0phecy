How to "Charge" the Clicker:
  1. Sit with your dog with 15 pea-sized high-value treats.
  2. Click, then immediately toss a treat. (Click $\to$ Treat).
  3. Repeat 15 times with zero verbal commands. When your dog's head snaps toward your hand at the sound of the click, the marker is conditioned!
