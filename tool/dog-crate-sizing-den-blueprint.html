The Potty Training Divider Panel Secret: If you buy an adult-sized crate for a puppy, you must install a divider panel! If a puppy has too much extra space, their natural instinct not to soil their bed fails—they will pee in one corner and sleep comfortably in the other. Give them just enough room to stand, turn around, and lie flat. Move the panel back as they grow!
