Music Theory Facts

  • 🎹 Major chords: Root + major third + perfect fifth (e.g. C–E–G = bright/happy)
  • 🎵 Minor chords: Root + minor third + perfect fifth (e.g. A–C–E = sad/melancholic)
  • 🎼 Seventh chords: Add a seventh note for jazz/soul (e.g. C–E–G–B♭)
  • ✨ Tip: Try C, E, G, B for a Cmaj7 (dreamy sound)
