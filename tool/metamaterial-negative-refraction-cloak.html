🔮 Metamaterials & Negative Index Cloaking Device

In standard materials, the refractive index is always positive ($n > 0$). But engineered metamaterials achieve negative permittivity ($\epsilon < 0$) and permeability ($\mu < 0$), reversing Snell's Law ($n < 0$) and bending light backward.
