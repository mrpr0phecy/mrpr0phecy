🌞 Sundial Designer

🌞 Sundial Designer

A sundial only works at the latitude it was cut for, which is why the garden-centre ones are decorative rather than accurate. Give it your latitude and this lays out the hour lines properly — the angles come straight from spherical trigonometry — sizes the gnomon, and tells you how many minutes to add today so the dial agrees with your watch.
