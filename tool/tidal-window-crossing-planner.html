🌊 Tidal Window & Crossing Planner

🌊 Tidal Window & Crossing Planner

Two kinds of people watch the tide: sailors who need enough water under the keel, and everyone crossing a causeway, ford or sandbar who needs the water gone. Give it one high water and one low water from any almanac and it builds the curve, marks your safe window to the minute, and shows how badly the old rule of twelfths would have misled you.
