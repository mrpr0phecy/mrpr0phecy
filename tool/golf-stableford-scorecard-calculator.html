⛳ Golf Stableford Points Calculator

Enter your course handicap and your gross score for each hole, and this works out the shots you receive, your net score, and the Stableford points per hole — the friendly format where a blob costs nothing and a par or better pays.

Scoring & shots received

Shots are shared out by stroke index: every hole gets your handicap ÷ 18, and the hardest holes (stroke index 1 up to your handicap's remainder) get one extra. Net score = gross − shots. Points: net double-bogey or worse 0, bogey 1, par 2, birdie 3, eagle 4, albatross 5.
