📷 Hyperfocal & Depth of Field

Hyperfocal distance H = f² / (N × c) + f. Focus there and everything from H/2 to infinity is acceptably sharp. Circle of confusion defaults: full frame 0.030 mm, APS-C 0.019 mm, Micro Four Thirds 0.015 mm.
