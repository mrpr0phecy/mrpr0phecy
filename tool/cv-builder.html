💡 Professional CV Tips
  • Use action verbs: Start bullet points with words like "Led", "Improved", "Managed", "Created"
  • Quantify achievements: Use numbers and percentages to show impact
  • Keep it to 1-2 pages: Recruiters spend only 6-8 seconds on initial review
  • Customize for each job: Mirror keywords from the job description
  • Proofread carefully: Typos and grammar errors are often immediate rejections
🎯 Career Strategy
Your CV is a marketing document - it should highlight your value, not just list duties.
