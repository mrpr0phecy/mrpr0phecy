🩺 Exercise Prescription & Physiology:
The 7-Second Asphalt Test: Place the back of your bare hand flat on the sunny pavement for 7 seconds. If it's too hot for your hand, it is hot enough to blister and melt your dog's sensitive paw pads! Walk strictly on grass or wait until sunset.
