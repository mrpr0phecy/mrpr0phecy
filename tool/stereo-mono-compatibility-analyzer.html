Stereo & Mono Compatibility Analyzer

Measure stereo correlation, side energy, and mono fold-down loss from your mix without uploading it.

Choose an audio file to begin.

Correlation is a whole-file, uncentered L/R energy measurement, not a time-varying meter. Mono is (L + R) / 2; negative correlation suggests cancellation, not automatically a bad mix. Decoding may resample to the browser’s audio rate.
