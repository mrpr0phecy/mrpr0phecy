📚 Kitchen Measurement Conversions
Volume Conversions
  • 1 cup = 240 ml = 16 tbsp
  • 1 tablespoon = 15 ml = 3 tsp
  • 1 teaspoon = 5 ml
  • 1 fluid ounce = 30 ml
  • 1 pint = 2 cups = 480 ml
  • 1 quart = 4 cups = 960 ml
Weight Conversions
  • 1 ounce = 28.35 grams
  • 1 pound = 16 oz = 454 grams
  • 1 kilogram = 1000 grams = 2.2 lbs
  • 1 stick butter = ½ cup = 113g
  • Common: Flour ≈ 120g/cup
  • Sugar ≈ 200g/cup, Brown ≈ 220g/cup
