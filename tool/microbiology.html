📚 Microbiology Concepts Explained
📈 Growth Rate
r = (ln(Nₜ/N₀)) / t
Where N₀ = initial cells, Nₜ = final cells, t = time
⏱️ Doubling Time
Tₐ = ln(2) / r
Time for population to double at growth rate r
🧴 Dilution Factor
DF = V₁ / V₂
Ratio of initial volume to final volume
🧫 CFU Calculation
CFU/mL = (colonies × dilution factor) / volume plated
