🦃 Turkey Thaw & Cook Time

🦃 Turkey Thaw & Cook Time

Fridge thaw ~24 h per 2–2.5 kg (FSA: 24 h per 2 kg is a safe extra). Oven times are a starting point — always probe 75 °C in the thickest thigh and stuffing. Not a substitute for the label on your bird.
