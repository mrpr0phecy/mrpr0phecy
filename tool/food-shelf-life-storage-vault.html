⚠️ Spoilage Signs vs Safe Changes: Danger Zone: 2h Max
Raw poultry spoiled: sour odor, slimy surface film, grayish cast. Discard immediately; bacteria will not be neutralized safely by high heat cooking.
Freezer Burn Prevention: Wrap tightly in heavy aluminum foil or vacuum seal. While food stored continuously at 0°F (-18°C) remains safe forever microbiologically, quality drops as sublimation occurs.
