🪴 Houseplant Matchmaker

The dating app for you and a plant. Answer honestly about your light, your watering habits and your pets, and it will set you up with a houseplant that can actually survive you.

🎭 Drama you can handle3/5
Methodology (plant dating science)

Matches are scored on light needs, watering tolerance and pet toxicity against your answers. If your answers say “cave + never water + pets”, the app will gently recommend a very resilient plant (or, failing that, a nice lamp).
