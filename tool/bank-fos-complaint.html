⚖️ Financial Ombudsman Complaint Builder

⚖️ Financial Ombudsman Complaint Builder

Build a complete FOS complaint — step by step. Get your money back when the bank refuses to help

How FOS Works
1. Bank must respond within 8 weeks
2. If unsatisfied → complain to FOS
3. FOS investigates (free for consumers)
4. FOS decision is binding on the bank
5. Can award up to £430,000

FOS Contact
🌐 financial-ombudsman.org.uk
📞 0800 023 4567
✉️ complaint.info@financial-ombudsman.org.uk

What FOS Can Award
• Refund of charges/fees
• Compensation for distress
• Compensation for inconvenience
• Interest on amounts owed
• Correction of credit file
• Payment for financial loss

Tips for Strong Complaints
• Include all dates and reference numbers
• Attach bank statements as evidence
• Be specific about amounts
• State which rules/laws were broken
• Keep copies of everything
