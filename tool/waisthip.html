How it works

Ratio = Waist ÷ Hip.
For men: Low risk <0.9, Moderate 0.9–0.99, High ≥1.0.
For women: Low risk <0.8, Moderate 0.8–0.89, High ≥0.9.
Based on WHO guidelines. Not a medical diagnosis.
