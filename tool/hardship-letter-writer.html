✉️ Hardship Letter Writer: Ask for Breathing Space

Falling behind on rent, energy or debts? Silence makes everything worse. Build a calm, formal hardship letter with a budget summary that asks for a payment plan, freeze or reduction — in UK, US or plain-international wording.

2 · Your details

Creditors say yes far more often when the numbers are attached. Be honest — a true budget beats a hopeful one.
