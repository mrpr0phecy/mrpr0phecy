Presentation Word Budget Planner

Presentation Word Budget Planner

Fit your talk to its slot: reserve time for questions and pauses, calculate a speaking word budget, then check your draft against it.

Words are counted as whitespace-separated tokens, so estimates suit space-separated languages. Speaking rates vary: rehearse aloud and allow extra time for slides, demonstrations and audience reactions.
