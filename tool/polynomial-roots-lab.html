Durand–Kerner (the “Weierstrass method”) updates all roots at once: zᵢ ← zᵢ − p(zᵢ)/∏ᵢ≠ⱼ(zᵢ−zⱼ). Started from a complex spiral it almost never lands in a degenerate symmetric trap; ten Newton polish steps tidy the tail. Multiple roots converge slowly — watch how long the double-root preset takes.
