🌤️ Migration & Thermal Lift Calc

Predict hawk watches, songbird flyways, and coastal fallouts. Evaluates synoptic frontal boundaries, barometric rise, wind vectors, and solar thermal lift.

Forecasted Flight Index: 95% — EPIC FLIGHT
The Fallout Phenomenon: When millions of nocturnal songbirds migrating across oceans or lakes encounter sudden headwind rainstorms, their energy is exhausted. They drop into the first coastal vegetation they see. A rainy front on the coast produces a legendary "Fallout" where trees drip with hundreds of warblers.
