⏳ Rule of 72 Doubling Time

⏳ Rule of 72 Doubling Time

Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ annual %. At 6% that’s about 12 years; at 9% about 8. The exact figure is ln(2)/ln(1+r). A back-of-envelope for interest, inflation and population — not a guarantee.
