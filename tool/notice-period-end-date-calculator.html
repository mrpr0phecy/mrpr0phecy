📅 Notice Period End Date

📅 Notice Period End Date

Add weeks or months to the day you hand in notice. UK statutory minimums are 1 week after a month’s service and 1 week per year after two years (capped at 12) — contracts are often longer. This is a calendar, not HR advice.
