🔬 Microbial Succession Timeline: 3 to 4 Weeks
Days 1–3: Initiation
Leuconostoc mesenteroides thrives, produces CO₂ and drops pH to 4.5.
Days 4–10: Lactic Acid
Lactobacillus plantarum takes over, accelerating souring and preservation.
Days 14+: Maturation
L. brevis & pentoaceticus develop complex funk and deep lactic tang.
Food Safety Rule: Always keep solids fully submerged under brine with glass fermentation weights. Add a bay leaf or grape leaf (tannins inhibit pectinase enzyme to maintain crunch).
