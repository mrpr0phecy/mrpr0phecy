Tsundere (ツンデレ)

Tsun (harsh) + Dere (lovey-dovey)
Acts aggressive, prickly, or outwardly hostile to conceal intense romantic embarrassment or vulnerability, softening as trust builds.
Catchphrase: "B-Baka! It's not like I like you or anything!"
Iconic Characters: Taiga, Asuka, Rin Tohsaka, Kaguya
