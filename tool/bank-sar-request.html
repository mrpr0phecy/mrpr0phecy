🔍 Subject Access Request (SAR) Generator

Force your bank to reveal ALL data they hold on you — internal notes, decision records, credit scoring, and communications

Request all data including:
Your Rights (GDPR / UK DPA 2018)
• Right to access ALL personal data
• Bank must respond within 1 calendar month
• Free of charge (unless manifestly unfounded)
• Must provide in commonly used format
• Includes internal notes, decisions, recordings

What You'll Get
• Transaction history
• Internal notes about your account
• Credit scoring decisions & factors
• Call recordings & chat logs
• Letters and emails sent/received
• Third parties data was shared with
• Fraud/AML flags on your account

Why This Matters
Banks often have internal notes that reveal:
• Why they really closed your account
• What they told credit agencies
• Decisions made by algorithms
• Complaints they tried to hide

Send To
Bank's Data Protection Officer
(Find on their website under Privacy Policy)
