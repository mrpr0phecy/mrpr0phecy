LI Explained
Land Impact = max(Physics, Download, Server) cost. Mesh objects with high physics cost or many triangles use more LI. Use Physics Shape Type: None on decorative objects to reduce LI.

Tips to Reduce LI
• Use mesh instead of prims
• Set physics to None on decorative items
• Reduce triangle count in Blender
• Combine small objects into one mesh
• Use LOD (Level of Detail) wisely
