How recurring dates work and where your data lives

When you mark a repeating task complete, its next due date is calculated from the date you completed it—not the old due date—so missed cycles do not stack up. Month-end dates are clamped to the last day of the new month. One-off tasks move to Completed and can be reopened.

Tasks and completion history are stored in this browser's local storage, not uploaded. Anyone using this browser profile may be able to see them. Use the backup for transfer; clear local data to remove the saved copy. This tool is a checklist, not a substitute for manufacturer, tenancy or local safety requirements.
