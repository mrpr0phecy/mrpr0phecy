🎓 Musical Terms Reference
Tempo Terms
• Largo: Very slow (40-60 BPM)
• Adagio: Slow (66-76 BPM)
• Andante: Walking pace (76-108 BPM)
• Allegro: Fast (120-168 BPM)
• Presto: Very fast (168-200 BPM)
Change Terms
• Accelerando: Gradually faster
• Ritardando: Gradually slower
• Rubato: Flexible/expressive time
• Subito: Sudden change
• A tempo: Return to original tempo
Duration Calculations:
• Beat Duration = 60 ÷ BPM (seconds)
• Section Duration = (Measures × Beats per measure) × Beat Duration
• Total Duration = Sum of all section durations
Dynamic Markings (Loudness):
• pp (Pianissimo): Very soft
• p (Piano): Soft
• mp (Mezzo Piano): Moderately soft
• mf (Mezzo Forte): Moderately loud
• f (Forte): Loud
• ff (Fortissimo): Very loud

⚠️ Educational Note: This tool provides tempo calculations and planning assistance. Actual musical performance requires artistic interpretation and should be guided by a qualified teacher or conductor. Use this as a starting point for composition and analysis.
