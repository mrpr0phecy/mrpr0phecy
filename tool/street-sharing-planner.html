🏘️ Street Sharing Planner

🏘️ Street Sharing Planner

Neighbourly sharing usually fails for two unglamorous reasons: nobody wants to be the one who knocks on a door first, and nobody knows who has what. This builds the sheet and writes the awkward messages for you. Everything stays in your browser — print the sheet or screenshot it into a group chat.

Tip: start with five offers before a single need. Streets respond much better to generosity than to requests, and once someone has taken something they are far more likely to ask.

Add a few offers and needs and I will match them, then write the messages you would otherwise never send.
Why this works better than a group chat

A street WhatsApp group goes quiet because asking in public risks being turned down in public. A printed sheet on a noticeboard, or a message sent to one person, has no audience. It also fixes the timing problem: offers written down can be claimed on Tuesday for something needed on Saturday.

Two ground rules that keep it going. First, keep it small — a dozen houses beats a hundred, because people need to recognise each other's names. Second, never let it become a debt ledger. The moment someone keeps score, sharing stops.
