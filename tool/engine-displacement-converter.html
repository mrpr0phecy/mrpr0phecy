🛠️ Engine Displacement

Convert litres, cubic centimetres and cubic inches (CID). 1 L = 1,000 cc; 1 in³ = 16.387064 cm³. A “5.0” V8 is 5,000 cc ≈ 305 CID. Bore × stroke × cylinders is the geometric displacement, not power.
