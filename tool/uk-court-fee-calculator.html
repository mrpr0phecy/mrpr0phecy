🏛️ UK Court, Tribunal & Probate Fee Calculator

Work out court fees for issuing a claim, the hearing fee by track, applications, appeals, enforcement, assessment and probate — current EX50 fees, uplifted 13 July 2026.

Nothing you enter leaves your device — the whole calculation runs in your browser.

A — Claim issue and hearing fees
B — Probate and employment tribunal

Employment tribunal claims and hearings are free — fees were abolished on 26 July 2017 after R (UNISON) v Lord Chancellor [2017] UKSC 51.

Full EX50 fee tables used here

Issue fee — money claims (fees order 1.1–1.8). Up to £300: £35 · £300.01–£500: £50 · £500.01–£1,000: £70 · £1,000.01–£1,500: £80 · £1,500.01–£3,000: £115 · £3,000.01–£5,000: £205 · £5,000.01–£10,000: £455 · £10,000.01–£200,000: 5% of the value · over £200,000: £10,000. Interest counts towards the value.

Possession and other non-money claims. Possession, County Court: £415 · possession, High Court: £559 · other non-money, County Court: £387 · other non-money, High Court: £663.

Hearing fees (fees order 2.1). Small claims: £27 (up to £300) · £59 (to £500) · £85 (to £1,000) · £123 (to £1,500) · £181 (to £3,000) · £346 (over £3,000). Fast track: £619. Intermediate and multi-track: £1,334. The hearing fee is payable when the court allocates, and a case that settles before the hearing does not pay it.

Applications (fees order 2.4–2.8). On notice: £321 · by consent or without notice: £126 · vary or extend a harassment injunction: £63 · payment out of court funds: £63 · certificate of satisfaction: £19 · vary or suspend a judgment: £16 · witness summons: £4.

Appeals (fees order 2.2–2.3). Appellant’s notice, High Court: £302 · County Court, small claims track: £151 · County Court, other claims: £175.

Enforcement (fees orders 7 and 8). Warrant of control £96 · warrant of delivery or possession £152 · further attempt at a new address £38 · writ of control, delivery or possession £82 · order for a debtor to attend to provide information £69 · bailiff service of a questioning order £139 · attachment of earnings £139 · charging order £139 · third-party debt order £139 · judgment summons £139 · enforcing another tribunal’s award in the County Court £55 · enforcing an award in the High Court £82.

Detailed assessment (fees order 5.2). Up to £15,000: £398 · £15,000.01–£50,000: £801 · £50,000.01–£100,000: £1,192 · £100,000.01–£150,000: £1,595 · £150,000.01–£200,000: £1,992 · £200,000.01–£300,000: £2,988 · £300,000.01–£500,000: £4,980 · over £500,000: £6,640.

Probate. Application fee for an estate over £5,000: £526 (no fee at or below £5,000) · extra sealed copies ordered with the application: £2 each · copies ordered later: £16 each · a second application after a grant: £22. The fee is a flat charge and is separate from any Inheritance Tax.

Online issue. Money Claim Online (MCOL) and Secure Data Transfer accept money claims up to £99,999.99; the Online Civil Money Claims (OCMC) service is for claims up to £10,000. Above those limits the claim is issued on paper or through CE-File.

Help with fees. Means-tested remissions and part-remissions are available (form EX160, or the online service); a fee can also be refunded if the claim settles before allocation or the hearing fee is no longer required. Fees here are from the EX50 tables uplifted on 13 July 2026 and checked on GOV.UK on 4 October 2026 — verify before paying, because fees change. — Information only, not legal advice.
