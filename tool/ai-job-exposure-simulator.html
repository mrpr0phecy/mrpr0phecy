Will AI Take My Job? — Task Exposure Simulator

Break a role into the work you actually do. Estimate what AI could assist with, what still needs a person, and how your result changes under stronger-capability assumptions. Your ratings—not a hidden database—drive every number.

Exposure is not job-loss probability. This models tasks, not whether a role or person will disappear. It has no employer, labour-market or future-AI data; hiring, adoption, law, cost, accountability and demand are outside the maths. Treat the result as a conversation starter, not career or financial advice.

Method, privacy and limits

Per task: AI-assisted hours = hours × your AI-capability rating. Potentially automatable hours = hours × your AI-capability rating × (1 − your human-essential share). The three sensitivity cards apply 1.00×, 1.25× and 1.50× to your capability ratings, capped at 100%; they do not change the human-essential share.

The example is invented to demonstrate the arithmetic, not an occupational study. Your tasks are held in volatile page memory and disappear when you leave. “Download private backup” saves the role and task labels you entered; protect that file. Copying the share text excludes task names and excludes your role title unless you opt in. Nothing is sent to a server.
