Pomodoro Technique
1. Study for 25 minutes (one pomodoro)
2. Take a 5 minute break
3. After 4 pomodoros, take a 15-30 min break
4. Repeat!

Study Tips
• Put your phone on DND
• One task at a time
• Write down distractions for later
• Reward yourself after each session
• Stay hydrated 💧
