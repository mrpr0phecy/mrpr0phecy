How Oven Tetris resolves temperature & shelf clashes

Why roast potatoes go soggy when cooked alongside the turkey

A whole turkey roasts gently at 180 °C fan and releases continuous steam into the cavity, whereas crisp roast potatoes and Yorkshire puddings need dry 210–215 °C heat. Crowding both into one oven steams the potatoes and dries the breast meat.

The 45-minute thermal rest secret

A 4–6 kg roast bird or joint insulated loosely under a double layer of foil and two clean dry tea towels holds piping-hot carving temperature for 40–55 minutes while muscle fibres relax and reabsorb juices. Pulling the centrepiece out 45–50 minutes before serving vacates the entire oven so you can turn the dial straight up to 210 °C fan for roasties, pigs in blankets, parsnips and Yorkshire puddings.

Shelf physics inside a fan oven

Even in a fan-assisted cavity, heat rises when trays block airflow: put Yorkshire puddings or roast potatoes on the top shelf, parsnips and pigs in blankets in the middle, and stuffing on the lower shelf. Rotate potato trays halfway if feeding more than 8 diners.
