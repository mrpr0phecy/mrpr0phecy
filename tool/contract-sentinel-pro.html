Method, limits & disclaimer

Pattern matcher over the raw text — 42 regex families for renewal, liability, indemnity, IP, confidentiality, non-compete, payment, termination, governing law, force majeure, assignment, warranties, etc. Each hit is scored by severity and by your role (e.g., unlimited liability is critical for a freelancer, less so for a buyer). Missing-clause detection looks for absent protections by keyword absence. This is educational triage, not legal advice. Always have a qualified lawyer review before signing.
