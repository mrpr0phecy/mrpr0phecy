💡 Lumens ⇄ Lux

💡 Lumens ⇄ Lux

Lux = lumens ÷ area (m²) when light is spread evenly. A 800 lm bulb over a 10 m² desk is 80 lux — offices often want 300–500 lux. Beam angle and bounce are ignored.
