🦕 The Scale Hall

Lengths and masses as the casts in the hall give them — mid-range figures from the current literature, not movie posters. Enter your height; the gold line is you, standing at the snout.

Lengths are snout-to-tail of large adults; masses are volumetric estimates and wander by tens of percent between papers. Periods follow the ICS. Velociraptor here is the Mongolian wolf-sized animal, not the Jurassic Park stand-in (that was closer to Deinonychus / Utahraptor).
