Feeder Hygiene & Disease Prevention: To prevent lethal outbreaks of Salmonella and Finch Conjunctivitis (Mycoplasma), wash feeders every 2 weeks in a 1:9 bleach-to-water solution (1 part bleach, 9 parts water). Rinse thoroughly, dry completely before refilling, and sweep wet rotting seed hull waste below.
