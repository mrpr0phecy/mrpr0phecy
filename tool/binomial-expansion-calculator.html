🧮 Binomial Expansion Calculator — (a + b)ⁿ

Expand any binomial such as (2x + 3)⁵ or (x − 1/y)⁸ term by term, with Pascal's triangle, the binomial coefficients, a “find term k” locator and Newton's generalised (1 + x)ⁿ approximation for fractional and negative powers.

Type each term as a plain monomial — coefficient, letter and power: 2x, -3y^2, x^5, 0.5t^3, or just 4 for a constant. Exponents may be negative (b = y^-1).
