✈️ Wind Triangle & Flight Leg Planner

✈️ Wind Triangle & Flight Leg Planner

Air is a moving floor. Point the nose where you want to go and the wind quietly carries you somewhere else, so every navigation log starts with the same triangle. This solves it exactly — heading to fly, drift, groundspeed, leg time, fuel, the point of no return, the crosswind on your chosen runway — and draws the vectors so you can see why the return leg never gives back what the outbound took.
