⚡ Speed Reading Trainer

Measure your words-per-minute, then train with guided pacing where your eye is told where to look. Includes recall questions and live WPM tracking. Nothing is stored online.

0 / 0 words 0 WPM
The method
Slow, average readers sub-vocalise and regress (look back). Training fixes two things at once: pacing — a fixed word-per-minute cadence so you can’t dawdle — and anchoring — the marker line keeps your eye moving forward. Do 3 passes: first read normally, then at pace, then read at pace again and take the recall test. Your reading rate should climb while recall stays around 70–80%.

Fair test: the timer covers the whole session from Start to finish — pause counts against you but the clock stops the moment the final word shows.
