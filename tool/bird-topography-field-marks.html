🔍 Avian Anatomy & Field Marks

Standard ornithological avian topography map. Learn precise feather tracts, facial stripes, wing coverts, and bill structures to nail difficult field identifications.

Supercilium ("Eyebrow Stripe") HEAD TRACT
Field ID Application: Separates Red-eyed Vireo (white supercilium bordered by black) from Philadelphia Vireo, and Tennessee Warbler from Orange-crowned Warbler.
Field Notebook Tip: When logging an unknown rarity in the field, always record the "Big 4" field marks before checking your field guide app: (1) Bill shape & color, (2) Eyering or facial stripes, (3) Presence of wing bars, and (4) Tail shape & outer feather contrast.
