🧹 After the Fireworks Safety Checklist

Know what to do after a display, including what to do if you find a suspected unexploded firework.

Do not attempt to relight, move or dispose of a suspected unexploded firework. This checklist does not replace the organiser’s instructions.

Reference: London Fire Brigade: fireworks safety.
