☀️ Quantum Tunneling: Why the Sun Actually Shines

According to classical physics, the Sun should not shine! Its core temperature (15 million Kelvin) provides only ~1 keV of kinetic energy—ten times too weak to overcome the 1,000 keV electrostatic Coulomb repulsion barrier between protons. Fusion only occurs because quantum protons act as waves that tunnel directly through the energy barrier.

15.0 Million K
Proton Kinetic Energy: 1.29 keV
Coulomb Barrier Peak: ~1,000.0 keV
Gamow Tunnel Probability: ~1 in 10²⁸
☀️ The Magic of the Gamow Peak: In a classical universe, protons would bounce off the barrier forever (a dead dark Sun). In quantum mechanics, the wavefunction decays exponentially through the wall and fuses on the inside, generating sunlight!
