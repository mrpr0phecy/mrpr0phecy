❤️ QTc Interval (Bazett)

❤️ QTc Interval (Bazett)

QTc (Bazett) = QT ÷ √(RR), with RR = 60 / heart-rate. Also shows Fridericia (QT ÷ RR⅓). Educational arithmetic from numbers you type — not an ECG, not a diagnosis. Chest pain or syncope needs emergency care.
