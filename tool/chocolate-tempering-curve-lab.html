🍫 Chocolate Tempering Curves

Cocoa butter polymorphism & Beta-V crystallization guide. Calculates 3-stage thermal curve temperatures, 80/20 seeding ratios, and diagnoses fat bloom versus clean snap.

1. Melt Phase
50°C / 122°F
Melts all crystal forms
2. Cool / Seed
28°C / 82°F
Forms Beta-V seed matrix
3. Working Temp
31.5°C / 89°F
Ready to mold / dip
⚖️ 80/20 Seeding Method Allocation: Form V Crystals
🥣 Melted Base (80%): 400 g
🍫 Finely Chopped Seed (20%): 100 g
Stir seed vigorously into melted base at 45°C until temperature drops to Phase 2. Seed introduces billions of stable Form V crystal templates.
🔬 Crystallization & Bloom Troubleshooting:
Fat Bloom (Streaks / Softness):
Occurs when chocolate was poured too warm (>33°C). Cocoa butter separates and recrystallizes in unstable Form IV.
Sugar Bloom (Grainy / Moisture):
Occurs from condensation or wet utensils. Water dissolves surface sugar; drying leaves rough white crystals.
The 3-Minute Parchment Test: Dip the tip of a paring knife or spatula into the tempered chocolate and leave at room temp (20°C). Properly tempered chocolate sets firm, satiny, and streak-free in 3 to 5 minutes.
