    Why plain flour turns to glue on wet socks — and why RSPB warns against glitter

    Never dust carpets with wheat flour or real fireplace soot: Wheat flour contains gluten; if a child steps on a flour footprint in damp slippers or spills Bucks Fizz, it forms sticky dough paste inside carpet fibres. Real chimney soot contains oily carbon that permanently greys wool pile. Instead, sieve 100% dry bicarbonate of soda (baking soda) over a cardboard stencil on carpets — it stays bright snowy white, neutralises pet odours, and lifts out in 15 seconds with a dry vacuum cleaner! On dark hardwood or slate tiles, a light mist of cornflour wipes clean with a dry microfibre cloth.

    Wildlife-safe outdoor "reindeer food": RSPB and wildlife rescues strongly advise never scattering plastic craft glitter or drieduncooked rice on lawns — microplastics stick to damp berries eaten by robins, thrushes and hedgehogs. Mix rolled porridge oats with sunflower hearts and a pinch of paprika or dried cranberries for genuine wintry sparkle that garden birds can safely eat on Christmas morning.

    Spot-test delicate silk/viscose rugs first; always vacuum dry powder before mopping. Last reviewed: 2026-11-12.
