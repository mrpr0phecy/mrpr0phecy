🌙 The 3am Worry Sorter

🌙 The 3am Worry Sorter

For the thoughts that queue up at 3am. Tick what's rattling around, add your own, and this will sort each one into Do Now, Schedule, or Let Go — with a kind word for each. Nothing is saved.

Methodology (a gentle algorithm)

Each worry is bucketed by the classic 3am test: can you act on it in the next two minutes (do now), can you give it a date and time (schedule), or is it not yours, not real yet, or not useful right now (let go). This is a thinking aid, not a therapist — if a worry is about your safety or health, please reach out to someone who can actually help.
