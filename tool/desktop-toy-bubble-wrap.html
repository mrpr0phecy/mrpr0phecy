🫧 Bubble Wrap Popper

Satisfying bubble wrap popping — click to pop, hold to drag-pop entire rows, shake to reset

0 / 0 popped
How it works
Each bubble has its own inflation physics. When you press, the bubble deforms realistically before popping with a satisfying burst animation. Nearby bubbles wobble from the shockwave.

Controls
• Click/tap — pop a single bubble
• Click & drag — pop a trail of bubbles
• New Sheet — fresh bubble wrap
• Pop all bubbles for a celebration!
