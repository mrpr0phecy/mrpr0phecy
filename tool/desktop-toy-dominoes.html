🀄 Domino Chain Reaction

🀄 Domino Chain Reaction

Place dominoes in a line, tap the first one, and watch the satisfying chain reaction cascade

How it works
Each domino is a rigid body with rotation physics. When one falls into the next, it transfers momentum through contact forces. Dominoes can create surprising patterns and chain splits.

Controls
• Place mode — click to place dominoes
• Push mode — click a domino to push it
• Spiral — auto-place a spiral pattern
• Clear — remove all dominoes
