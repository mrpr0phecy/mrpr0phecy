🎓 Tic Tac Toe Strategy Guide
Opening Moves
• Center: Best opening move
• Corners: Second-best option
• Edges: Weakest opening
• Forks: Create two threats
Defensive Tactics
• Block: Prevent opponent's three-in-a-row
• Counter-attack: Create your own threat
• Center control: Dominates the board
• Corner defense: Strong defensive position
Perfect Play Rules:
1. Win: If you have two in a row, play the third
2. Block: If opponent has two in a row, block them
3. Fork: Create an opportunity where you have two ways to win
4. Block Fork: Force opponent into blocking instead of forking
5. Center: Play center if open
6. Opposite Corner: If opponent is in corner, take opposite corner
7. Empty Corner: Play empty corner
8. Empty Side: Play empty side
Game Theory Facts:
• Perfect play results in a draw - both players cannot lose if they play optimally
• 765 possible game states (not counting rotations and reflections)
• 26,830 possible games if both players play randomly
• First player has a slight advantage (wins about 58% of random games)
• Game is "solved" - computer algorithms can play perfectly
• M,n,k-game generalization - Tic Tac Toe is a 3,3,3-game

💡 Pro Tip: Against perfect play, Tic Tac Toe always ends in a draw. The challenge is to avoid making mistakes that give your opponent a winning opportunity. Practice recognizing patterns and anticipating your opponent's moves!
