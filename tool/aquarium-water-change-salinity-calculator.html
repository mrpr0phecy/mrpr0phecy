🧪 Water Change & Salinity

Precision aquatic water chemistry toolkit. Calculate exact nitrate dilution percentages, RO/DI remineralization buffers for dwarf shrimp and plants, and marine saltwater salt batch mixing.

Required Water Change
62.5%
Water To Drain & Refill
12.5 Gal (47.3 L)
Perform a 62.5% water change. If doing >50% change in one session, ensure new water is precisely temperature-matched (within ±1°C) and conditioned with dechlorinator before adding.
SaltyShrimp GH/KH+
3.8 grams
(~1.9 level scoops)
Seachem Equilibrium (GH)
7.5 grams
(~1.5 teaspoons)
Baking Soda NaHCO₃ (KH)
1.1 grams
(~0.25 teaspoon)
Target TDS estimate: ~180–210 ppm. Dissolve salts thoroughly in bucket with a small powerhead or airstone before adding to tank.
Reef Salt Mix Required
1,320 g (2.91 lbs)
Standard Cup Measure
~5.0 cups (~1/2 cup per gal)
💡 Golden Rule of Marine Evaporation: Water evaporates, but salt does NOT. Never top off an evaporating tank with saltwater; always top off daily with pure RO/DI freshwater to keep salinity locked at 1.025 SG.
