LUFS Target & Headroom Planner

LUFS Target & Headroom Planner

Use measured integrated LUFS and true peak to plan a gain change and see whether your chosen peak ceiling permits it.



Enter measurements from a trusted whole-track LUFS/true-peak meter. This tool does not measure audio or apply a limiter. Constant gain shifts LUFS and true peak by approximately the same dB amount; gating can change the result. Targets are your choice, not a universal platform requirement. Re-measure after processing.
