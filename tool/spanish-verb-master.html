🇪🇸 Spanish Verb Conjugator

Master Spanish verb conjugations, conquer the notorious Ser vs Estar dilemma (permanent essence vs temporary state), and master Por vs Para with native Spanish audio playback.

Both mean "To Be" — Here is the Infallible Rule:
SER (D.O.C.T.O.R.)
• Description (Soy alto)
• Occupation (Es músico)
• Characteristic (Son amables)
• Time / Date (Son las tres)
• Origin (Soy de Londres)
• Relationship (Ella es mi hermana)
ESTAR (P.L.A.C.E.)
• Position (Está sentado)
• Location (Estoy en casa)
• Action / -ing (Está comiendo)
• Condition (El café está frío)
• Emotion (Estamos felices)
Both mean "For" — How to Choose:
POR = Cause / Reason / Exchange
• Reason: Gracias por todo (Thanks for everything)
• Duration: Por dos horas (For two hours)
• Exchange: Pagué £20 por esto (I paid £20 for this)
• Means of transport: Por avión (By plane)
PARA = Goal / Recipient / Deadline
• Recipient: Esto es para ti (This is for you)
• Destination: Salgo para Madrid (Leaving for Madrid)
• Deadline: Para mañana (For tomorrow)
• In order to: Estudio para aprender (I study in order to learn)
