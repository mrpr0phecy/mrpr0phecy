🔢 Continued Fraction Explorer — The Hidden Skeleton of Numbers

Every number, rational or irrational, unfolds into a chain of whole numbers: a₀ + 1/(a₁ + 1/(a₂ + …)). Cut the chain early and you get the best fraction there is for the size — 355/113 for π, Pell equations solved for √d. Type a decimal, a fraction like 355/113, or sqrt(13), and watch the convergents snap shut on the value.
