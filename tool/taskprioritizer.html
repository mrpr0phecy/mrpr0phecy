💡 How to Use the Eisenhower Matrix
Quadrant 1: Do Now

Critical tasks that need immediate attention. High importance, high urgency.

Quadrant 2: Schedule

Important but not urgent. Plan these for focused work time.

Quadrant 3: Delegate

Urgent but not important. Consider delegating or minimizing.

Quadrant 4: Eliminate

Neither important nor urgent. Eliminate or minimize these.

Priority Score Formula:
Score = (Importance × 0.7) + (Urgency × 0.3)
Higher score = higher priority. Importance weighted 70%, urgency 30%.
