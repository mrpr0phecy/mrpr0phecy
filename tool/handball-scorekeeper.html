🤾 Handball Scorekeeper

Two halves, goals, and the two-minute suspensions that define the game. Tap to score, track each team's suspensions, and keep the running total.

The basics

Seven players a side, two 30-minute halves, one point per goal. A two-minute suspension leaves a team a player down — three suspensions for the same player is a red card. A 7-metre throw is awarded for a clear scoring chance denied by a foul.
