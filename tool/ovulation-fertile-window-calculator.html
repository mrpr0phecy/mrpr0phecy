🌸 Ovulation & Fertile Window

Estimate ovulation and the fertile window from the first day of your last period and your typical cycle length. Sperm can survive up to five days, so the window starts before ovulation. Calendar method only — not contraception, not medical advice.

Irregular cycles, illness, travel and stress all shift ovulation. Use ovulation tests or a clinician if you need accuracy.
