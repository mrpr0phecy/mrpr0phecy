🎳 Ten-Pin Bowling Score Calculator

🎳 Ten-Pin Bowling Score Calculator

Enter the pinfall for each roll and this scores the game for you — strikes and spares with the bonuses, and the tenth frame's extra balls, all handled automatically.

How scoring works

A strike (10 on the first roll) is worth 10 plus your next two rolls. A spare (10 across two rolls) is worth 10 plus your next roll. The tenth frame lets you roll up to three times so the bonuses can be added — two extra balls after a strike, one after a spare. A perfect game is 12 strikes for 300.
