🧪 Mock Data Generator

Create repeatable sample customer, product or analytics-event datasets as JSON, CSV or SQL inserts. Names and example.test emails are deliberately fictional, so you can prototype safely without uploading real records.

Keep the same seed and settings to reproduce the same fake records.
What makes this safe for demos?

This tool chooses from a small fictional word list and uses the reserved example.test domain, which cannot resolve on the public internet. It does not contact a database, collect a form or generate a real person’s profile. Treat generated data as test-only, not as a realistic demographic sample.
