🌡 Environmental Baseline Survey

Compare manually recorded baseline readings with a later observation at the same marked point. Use calibrated instruments, note their position, and report the raw difference instead of calling a change “paranormal.”

MANUAL MEASUREMENTS · NO SENSOR CLAIMS
Enter readings from your own instruments. The page does not sample temperature, humidity, sound, light, or magnetic fields. Changes need ordinary-source checks; dB is logarithmic and only comparable on the same meter/settings.
