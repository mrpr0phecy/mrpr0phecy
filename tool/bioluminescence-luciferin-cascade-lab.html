✨ Bioluminescent Luciferin Chemical Cascade Lab

Bioluminescence is the miracle of "cold light"—producing near 100% luminous efficiency with zero thermal waste. Discover how enzyme Luciferase oxidizes substrate Luciferin using cellular ATP, agitate dinoflagellate sea tides with water shear stress, and compare deep-sea oceanic emission spectra.

80%
LH₂ + ATP + O₂ —[Luciferase]→ Oxyluciferin + AMP + PPi + hν (Photon)
⚡ 0 Photons Emitted
