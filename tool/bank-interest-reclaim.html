Calculate how much you've been overcharged on overdraft interest and fees — then generate a reclaim letter

Can You Reclaim?
You may be able to reclaim overdraft charges if:
• Fees were disproportionate to the bank's costs
• You weren't clearly informed of the charges
• The bank didn't warn you before applying charges
• You were in financial difficulty at the time

FCA Rules (since 2020)
• Banks must charge a single EAR (not daily fees)
• Must show cost per £100 borrowed
• Must give tools to avoid overdraft
• Cannot charge more for unarranged overdrafts

How to Get Statements
1. Log into online banking
2. Download 6 years of statements
3. Or make a SAR for full records
4. Add up all overdraft-related charges

Success Rate
Banks often settle small claims to avoid court. Even partial refunds are common. Always try — you have nothing to lose.
