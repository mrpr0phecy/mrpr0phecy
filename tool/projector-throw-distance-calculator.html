📽️ Projector Throw Distance

📽️ Projector Throw Distance

Throw ratio = distance to screen ÷ image width. A 1.5:1 lens 3 m from the wall makes a 2 m wide picture. 16:9 assumed unless you change it. Check the projector’s own min/max zoom range.
