Why the "Acknowledge -> Bridge -> nostalgia question" formula always works

Never argue the premise at 3 PM over gravy

Pushing back head-on ("Actually you're wrong about...") locks two people into performance mode while six other relatives stare into their parsnips. A 3-second light acknowledgement followed immediately by an easy, slightly flattering nostalgia or opinion question ("Wait — before we lose the afternoon to the news, settle a bet for me: what was the single best toy you ever unwrapped as a kid?") gives everyone an off-ramp with dignity intact.
