🏃 Running Cadence & Stride Length

Cadence, pace and stride length are locked together — fix any two and the third is forced. Fill in the two you know and this computes the rest, plus your steps per kilometre and total steps for a distance.

The relationship & what to aim for

Speed = cadence × stride length, so at a fixed pace the two trade off. Most coaches nudge runners toward a higher cadence (around 170–180 steps/min) with a shorter, quicker stride — it tends to reduce overstriding and the braking force that comes with it.
