🔺 SL Mesh Upload Cost Calculator

Estimate Land Impact and upload cost for mesh objects — input triangle counts and physics shapes

LI Calculation
Land Impact = max(Download, Physics, Server) weight. Each component is calculated from triangle counts, LOD distances, and physics complexity.

Triangle Guidelines
Simple prop200–1K tris
Furniture1K–5K tris
Clothing5K–15K tris
Avatar body15K–40K tris
Detailed building5K–20K tris

Physics Tips
• None — 0 physics cost (decorative only)
• Prim — low cost, convex hull approximation
• Mesh — accurate but higher cost
• Use None for wall art, plants, decor
• Use Prim for most furniture
