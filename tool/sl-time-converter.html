SL Time = Pacific Time
Second Life runs on PST (UTC-8) or PDT (UTC-7) during daylight saving. All SL events, land auctions, and scheduled maintenance use SLT.

Common SL Event Times
Live music12–6 PM SLT
Fashion events10 AM–2 PM SLT
DJ parties4 PM–12 AM SLT
Grid maintenance6 AM SLT (Tues)

Daylight Saving
SL follows US Pacific DST:
• PDT (March–November): UTC-7
• PST (November–March): UTC-8
