Holiday tipping is always optional and never replace fair pay—a sincere handwritten card genuinely makes someone’s week. In the US, USPS mail carriers cannot accept cash or cash-equivalent gift cards under federal ethics rules (non-cash gifts up to $20 value are permitted), whereas private couriers vary. In the UK, many local councils restrict refuse crews from accepting cash, making sealed biscuit/chocolate tubs + a card taped to the bin lid the safest gesture.
