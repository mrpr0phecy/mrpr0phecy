📡 RF Link Budget & Fresnel Zone Planner

Will the link work? Radio needs more than line of sight — it needs a clear ellipse of space around the beam, and the Earth quietly bulges up into it. This adds up every gain and loss in the chain, draws the first Fresnel zone over a curved Earth, and tells you how many metres higher your masts need to be.
