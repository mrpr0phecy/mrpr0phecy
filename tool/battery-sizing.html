⚠️ Important Battery Safety & Facts
Formula: Ah = (W × h) / (V × Efficiency)
Amp-hours = (Watts × Hours) ÷ (Volts × Efficiency)
⚠️ Critical Safety
• Always consult qualified electricians
• Follow manufacturer datasheets
• Comply with local electrical codes
Battery Types
• Lead-acid: Cheap, heavy, 50% DoD
• Lithium: Light, expensive, 80% DoD
• Deep-cycle: Best for long runtime
Pro Tips
• Add 20-30% safety margin
• Consider temperature effects
• Factor in battery aging
• Include inverter losses
