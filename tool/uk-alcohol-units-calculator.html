🍺 UK Alcohol Units Calculator

🍺 UK Alcohol Units Calculator

UK units = ABV (%) × volume (ml) ÷ 1,000. The NHS low-risk guideline is 14 units a week, spread out, with several drink-free days. This is arithmetic, not medical advice.
