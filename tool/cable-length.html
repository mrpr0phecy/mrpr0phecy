⚠️ Electrical Safety & Best Practices
Formula: Vdrop = 2 × L × I × R / 1000
Voltage drop = 2 × Length(ft) × Current(A) × Resistance(Ω/kft)
⚠️ Critical Safety
• Always hire licensed electricians
• Follow NEC/ local codes
• Use proper circuit protection
AWG vs mm²
• 14 AWG = 2.08 mm² (15A max)
• 12 AWG = 3.31 mm² (20A max)
• 10 AWG = 5.26 mm² (30A max)
Pro Tips
• Calculate round-trip distance
• Factor in temperature rating
• Consider future expansion
• Use voltage drop tables
