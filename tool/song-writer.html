📚 Songwriting Facts

  • • Most pop songs follow a structure: Verse–Chorus–Verse–Chorus–Bridge–Chorus
  • • Lyrics should create emotional connection through imagery and metaphor
  • • The chorus should contain the main hook – the most memorable part
  • • Bridge sections provide contrast and prevent listener fatigue
  • ⚠️ Always verify techniques with a teacher or professional resource
