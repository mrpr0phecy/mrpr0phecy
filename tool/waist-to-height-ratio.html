📏 Waist-to-Height Ratio

📏 Waist-to-Height Ratio

NICE NG246 uses waist ÷ height as a central-adiposity screen for adults with BMI under 35. Measure the waist at the midpoint between the last rib and the hip bone. Not a diagnosis.
