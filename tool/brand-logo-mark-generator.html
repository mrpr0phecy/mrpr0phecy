Design a real logo in the browser and leave with the vector file. Twenty-two hand-drawn icons, five type treatments, four container shapes, gradient or flat colour, and exports as SVG, a 1024px PNG and a 64px favicon. No watermark, no account, no £20 logo-maker subscription — the SVG you download is yours.

Notes before you use this commercially

The icon geometry is drawn from original paths in this file, so the artwork is not copied from a stock set, but simple geometric marks are rarely protectable on their own — check your local trademark register before you rely on exclusivity. Exported SVG uses only <path>, <text>, <circle> and gradients, so it opens cleanly in Figma, Illustrator and Inkscape. If you convert the text to outlines before print you avoid the one real risk: the viewer not having your chosen system font installed.
