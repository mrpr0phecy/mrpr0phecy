⭐ Constellation Map

Interactive star map — explore 30 major constellations, click stars for names and facts

How to Use
• Click any star for its name and magnitude
• Use the dropdown to highlight one constellation
• Toggle lines and labels with checkboxes

Star Magnitude
Brighter stars have lower magnitudes:
• 1.0 — very bright (Sirius, Vega)
• 2.0-3.0 — easily visible
• 4.0-5.0 — faint, need dark sky
• 6.0+ — binoculars/telescope needed

Best Time to See
• Orion — Winter (Dec-Feb)
• Scorpius — Summer (Jun-Aug)
• Ursa Major — Year-round (N. hemisphere)
• Cassiopeia — Year-round (N. hemisphere)
