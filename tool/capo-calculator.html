💡 Capo Tips & Facts
Basic Principle
A capo raises pitch by one semitone per fret. Fret 1 = +1 semitone, Fret 2 = +2 semitones (whole step), etc.
Common Uses
• Change key without new chord shapes
• Match vocal range
• Create brighter tone
Capo Types
• Spring: Quick, one-handed
• Screw: Precise tension
• Strap: Even pressure
Pro Tips
• Place capo close to fret wire
• Check all strings ring clearly
• Adjust for different neck widths
