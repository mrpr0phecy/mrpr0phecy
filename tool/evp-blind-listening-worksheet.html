Capture an independent first-pass transcription before revealing the question or expected phrase. Then replay, record a second pass and compare the notes without the tool supplying words.

BLIND REVIEW · NO SPEECH RECOGNITION
The page does not transcribe or classify audio. Expectation can shape what listeners hear; keep the first pass separate, include “unclear” when appropriate, and never treat a perceived phrase as an instruction.
