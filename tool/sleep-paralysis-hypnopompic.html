🛡️ Sleep Paralysis Navigator

🛡️ Sleep Paralysis Navigator

Demystify REM motor atonia. Learn how glycine and GABA temporarily paralyze skeletal muscles, and either smoothly wake up or launch directly into a vivid lucid flight.

🔬 The Neurobiology Behind It:
During REM sleep, brainstem neurons release glycine and GABA to inhibit post-synaptic alpha motor neurons. This is a vital evolutionary safety mechanism called REM Atonia—it prevents you from physically sleepwalking or acting out dreams.

Crucially: Your heart, lungs, and eye muscles are NEVER paralyzed! Any chest pressure is caused by autonomic breathing rhythms, not physical restriction.
