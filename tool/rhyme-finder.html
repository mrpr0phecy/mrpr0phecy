🪞 Rhyme Finder

Find true rhymes and near rhymes for any word — powered by an embedded CMU pronunciation dictionary, no network needed.

How it works: pronunciation comes from CMUdict (embedded). A true rhyme shares the same sound from the last stressed vowel onwards (time, rhyme, climb). A near rhyme matches the final sound but not the stressed vowel (stone, tone vs gone, bone).
