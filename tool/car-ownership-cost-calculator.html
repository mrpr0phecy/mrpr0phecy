🚗 Car Ownership Cost Calculator (UK)

Work out the true cost of running a car in the UK — depreciation, fuel or electricity, insurance, VED road tax, servicing, MOT, tyres and repairs — per month and per mile. Everything calculates locally, no data leaves your device.

Your car

Hidden costs to remember: MOT (£40–£55 every year once the car is 3 years old), breakdown cover (£40–£150/yr), parking or permits (£200–£1,500+/yr in cities), and finance interest if you borrowed to buy. A cheap car is rarely cheap.
