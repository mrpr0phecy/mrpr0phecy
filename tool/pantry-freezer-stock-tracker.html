Privacy, saving and date notes

Entries are stored in this browser's local storage, not uploaded to a server. Anyone using this browser profile may be able to see them. Erase the list to remove its saved copy, or download a JSON backup first. The app does not send reminders while closed.

A use-by or best-before date is displayed exactly as entered; the tracker does not assess food quality or safety. For date-only entries, “within 7 days” includes dates that have already passed and dates today through seven days ahead.
