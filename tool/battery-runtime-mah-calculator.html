🔋 Battery Runtime (mAh)

🔋 Battery Runtime (mAh)

Hours ≈ (capacity mAh × (voltage / load voltage) × efficiency) / load mA. A 2000 mAh cell at 100 mA is about 20 hours at 100% — real packs are less because of cut-off voltage and converter loss.
