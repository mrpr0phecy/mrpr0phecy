Quick guide
• Noun — person, place, thing, idea: dog, London, courage
• Verb — action or state: run, is, think
• Adjective — describes a noun: happy, tall, red
• Adverb — describes a verb, adjective or adverb: quickly, very
• Pronoun — replaces a noun: he, she, it, they, who
• Preposition — position or relation: in, on, under, with
• Conjunction — joins words or clauses: and, but, because
• Interjection — sudden emotion: wow!, ouch!, hey!

Tip: many words can be more than one part depending on context — “light” can be a noun, verb or adjective. In these sentences only one answer fits.
