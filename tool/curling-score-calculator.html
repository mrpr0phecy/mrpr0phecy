🥌 Curling Score Calculator

In each end, the team with the stone closest to the button scores one point for every stone nearer than the opposition's best. Enter each end and this keeps the running score to the last stone.

The shot-rock rule

Only one team scores in an end — the one with the stone (the “shot rock”) resting closest to the button. It scores one point for every one of its stones that is closer to the button than the opponent's closest stone, up to a maximum of 8. The team that fails to score in an end keeps the hammer (last-stone advantage) for the next.
