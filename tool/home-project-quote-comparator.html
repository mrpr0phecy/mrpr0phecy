🧾 Home Project Quote Comparator

Compare up to three contractor estimates on a more like-for-like basis. Record each quote’s total, known extras, included work, exclusions, schedule, payment terms and guarantee notes—without pretending that the lowest number is automatically the best choice.

For a fair arithmetic comparison, enter each quote’s total payable including tax/fees, then add only known costs that are not already included.
