Methodology (feels-like science)

"Feels like" uses a simple wind-chill approximation so a 12°C day with a stiff breeze dresses like a 7°C day. Recommendations also account for duration and rain, because the correct coat for a two-minute bin run is a braver statement than the correct coat for a three-hour hike. Optimism is included as a correction factor with a straight face.
