💡 Business Insights & Tips
Lower Fixed Costs
Reducing fixed costs lowers break‑even point. Consider shared workspaces, remote teams, or renegotiating contracts.
Increase Price
Even small price increases significantly impact break‑even point if demand stays constant.
Reduce Variable Costs
Bulk purchasing, efficiency improvements, or supplier negotiation can lower per‑unit costs.
