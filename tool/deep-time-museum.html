The hall is the whole of Earth's history, compressed. Pick a chip to jump — or just scroll: every 64px of walk is about 14 million years.

Dated on the standard geological time scale (ICS). Exact figures vary by a few percent between sources — these are the accepted central estimates. Oldest Earth minerals ≈ 4.40 Ga (Jack Hills zircons); Earth itself 4.54 Ga. First life and the earliest fossils remain open scientific questions, and are labelled as such here.
