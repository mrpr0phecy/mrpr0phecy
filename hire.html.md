## Questions

**How do I know you'll actually deliver?**

You pay a deposit to start and the balance only after you've clicked a working demo. If the demo isn't right, we iterate; if I can't build what I quoted, the deposit comes back. The whole site is also public proof I finish things.

**Who owns the custom tool?**

You own your branding, wording and any of your content in it, and you get the embed for your site. The underlying code pattern may be reused or open-sourced — that's how the price stays fixed instead of agency-shaped. Exclusive buyout of the code is negotiable; ask.

**Will it track my visitors?**

No. Everything I build runs in the browser with zero third-party scripts, zero cookies and no lead-capture — unless you explicitly ask for an integration, and then it's disclosed, not hidden.

**Do you do rush jobs?**

Sometimes. Audits can usually turn around in 48 hours. Custom builds are 1–2 weeks normally; say the date in your first email and I'll tell you straight away whether it's possible.

**What won't you build?**

Gambling, crypto speculation, anything that harvests data quietly, anything that reads as professional advice (medical dosing, “invest in this”), and anything I can't test properly. I'd rather turn work down than ship something I'd be embarrassed to put my name on.
