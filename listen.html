Who's Behind It

MrProphecy is a one-person operation out of Luton. The writing, the production, the animation and the artwork all come from the same desk.

The result sits somewhere between UK hip hop and something more visual — tracks that are made to be watched as much as heard, which is why the animated work lives on YouTube rather than a streaming service.

There's a back catalogue on SoundCloud going back years, including Gain of Function. All of it is free.
