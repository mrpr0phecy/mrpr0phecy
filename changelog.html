  • 2 Sept 2026 · 1dd6d6e

    Embed any tool Feature

    New /embed page that lists all 562 tools with a one-click "Copy iframe" button. Filter by category. Every tool can be embedded on a third-party site with a single iframe — no JavaScript, no API key, no sign-up.

    • Per-tool embed snippet generated client-side; the snippet is live in the page when copied so it can be inspected before pasting.
    • Category filter with sticky position under the topbar.
    • Old-browser fallback: shows the snippet inline if navigator.clipboard is unavailable.
  • 2 Sept 2026 · c41c439

    About + Press Feature

    New /about page with project history, principles, stats, and milestones. New /press page with a one-paragraph factsheet, story angles for tech / consumer / music / indie press, contact details, and brand-asset notes.

    • Both pages include structured data (AboutPage, WebPage JSON-LD) for E-E-A-T and rich-result eligibility.
    • Cross-linked from every other top-level page in the footer pill row.
  • 2 Sept 2026 · 19c0bc4

    Use case + per-tool JSON-LD Feature SEO

    New /use-case page with 33 hand-curated "what are you trying to do?" buckets covering 271 of the 562 tools — the rest are still discoverable from /tools and /.

    Every tool loaded via /tool.html?card=... now injects its own per-tool WebApplication JSON-LD with the actual title, description, category, and the canonical standalone URL — replacing the generic placeholder. The page's <title>, og:title, og:description, twitter:*, and <link rel="canonical"> are all updated to match.

  • 2 Sept 2026 · e22e09e

    Discovery pages + RSS + machine-readable Feature SEO

    Four new discovery pages, one feed, three machine-readable files.

    • /popular — 60 hand-curated "most useful" tools across 7 categories, with ItemList JSON-LD for the top 30.
    • /new — the 10 tools added in the most recent catalogue-audit commit.
    • /tools — the complete index of all 562 tools organised by category, with a sticky in-page TOC.
    • /help — 20-question FAQ with FAQPage JSON-LD, sticky section nav, and a "suggest a tool" CTA.
    • /feed.xml — RSS 2.0 feed with items for each of the above.
    • /llms.txt — machine-readable overview for LLM crawlers (per the llmstxt.org spec).
    • /.well-known/ai.txt — explicit AI policy (training, retrieval, republishing rules).
    • /.well-known/security.txt — security disclosure address and expiry.
    • /robots.txt now explicitly allows the major AI crawlers (GPTBot, Claude-Web, ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Applebot-Extended, cohere-ai, cohere-training-data-crawler).
    • Every top-level page links to the RSS feed via <link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml">.
  • 2 Sept 2026 · cee0c8b

    +10 tools, 23 categories, 562 total Content

    After a full audit of the 552-tool catalogue, ten genuine coverage gaps were found and filled:

    • Hike Time Planner — Naismith + Langmuir descent + real sunset times from your latitude. (Home & DIY)
    • Bike Gear Calculator — gear inches, development, gain ratio, wheel duplicates. (Health & Fitness)
    • Photo Exposure Lab — EV equivalence, ND, true DOF (not the marketing version), diffraction limit. (Science & Engineering)
    • Race Pace Predictor — Riegel predictions for any distance, plus five training paces. (Health & Fitness)
    • Baby Sleep Planner — wake windows → nap length → bedtime, by age band. (Productivity & Lifestyle)
    • Energy Tariff Comparator — annual cost AND the usage where ranking flips. (Finance & Money)
    • Unit Price Comparator — normalises multi-buys to a single comparable number. (Finance & Money)
    • Fluid Type Scale — CSS clamp() solved from two breakpoints, in rem. (Productivity & Lifestyle)
    • Fabric Yardage Estimator — width, nap, pattern repeat, pre-wash shrinkage. (Science & Engineering)
    • Car Care Tracker — MOT + service countdowns, tread, cost per mile. (Science & Engineering)

    Spread across 5 existing categories (no new category invented for these). The full cards.json is regenerated; the other 552 cards have zero drift.
